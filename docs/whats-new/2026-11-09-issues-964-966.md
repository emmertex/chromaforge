### Fixes: Canon 7D framing, startup message, unreadable folders

- Canon EOS 7D raws open with their full 3:2 frame again instead of being cropped to 4:3.
- No more "no older install's presets" error message at startup on computers without an older install.
- A folder the app is not allowed to read (for example without macOS Files and Folders permission) now says so instead of showing an empty folder.
