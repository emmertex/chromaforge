### Plug-ins say where they come from

- A plug-in's About tab, and a pixel filter's page, show its homepage, source code and changelog as links named by the site they go to, and its licence. A plug-in whose source is on GitHub gets a *Report a Problem* link to its issues.
- Nothing is fetched to show them: a link opens in your browser only when you click it, and only `https://` links open.
