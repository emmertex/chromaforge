### Smart Masks presets
- A new built-in group, Smart Masks: Sun Kissed, Recover Blue Suit, Bring Sky Down, Brighten Dress and Darken
  Background. Each adds a mask selected on the photo it is applied to (the Groom's or Bride's clothes by role, the
  sky, the background, a gradient anchored to the people), so they stack on any look. Thanks @OneSlips.
