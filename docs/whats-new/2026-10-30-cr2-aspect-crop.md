### A CR2 opens in the aspect ratio set in the camera
- A Canon CR2 shot at an in-camera aspect ratio, such as 3:2 on a PowerShot with a 4:3 sensor, now opens in that shape, as a CR3 already did. The raw always holds the whole sensor image; the camera records the rectangle you framed in its maker note, and the app now uses it as the photo's default crop.
- Photos shot at the sensor's own shape are unchanged, and thumbnails are rendered again once. A crop or local adjustment made on such a photo before keeps its position relative to the frame, so on the smaller frame it covers a slightly different part of the picture.
