### Plug-ins can find and set a photo's crop
- With your permission, a plug-in can look at a downsized copy of a photo and set its crop and straighten angle: find a film frame inside its rebate, say, and crop and level it. The crop it sets is an ordinary one: Undo removes it, and the crop tool adjusts it.
- The Plug-in Manager asks for each of the two permissions on install: "Look at downsized copies of photos" and "Change the crop of photos".
