### Culling shows your edit at once
- A photo you have edited never shows the camera's own JPEG any more, not in the loupe, a grid cell or the filmstrip: until its render is there, its last rendered picture stands in (cut to the current crop when the look is close), else a quick draft of your edit made from the camera JPEG.
- Stepping through photos in the loupe shows the next one at once: the stand-ins for the next five photos in the direction you are going (and the one behind) are made ahead, and while you hold an arrow key the full render waits until you stop.
- Zoomed to 100 % on an unedited raw, the camera's JPEG at full size is shown until the window render arrives, so a focus check doesn't wait for the raw.
- While the loupe shows a raw's camera JPEG, a small "Camera preview" label says so: judge colour and tone on the rendered photo.
