### Point Color in masks: Visualize range, and effect presets keep it

- Saving a mask effect preset now keeps the mask's Point Color samples, and applying the preset gives them to the mask.
- Camera Raw local presets with Point Color samples bring them in instead of reporting them as unsupported.
- A mask's Point Color now has Visualize range: the selected sample's colours stay in colour and the rest of the photo turns grey.
