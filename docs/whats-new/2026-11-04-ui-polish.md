### UI polish
- The message after a library upgrade is titled "Library upgraded" instead of "Settings file problem".
- File ▸ Libraries is the one place for libraries: the Catalog submenu and Open Recent Catalog are gone, and a library listed twice under a slightly different path shows once.
- Import is in the top bar beside Library, Develop and the other modules, and the bar stays in place while you import.
- Messages in the title bar disappear after 15 seconds, or when you click them.
- Window ▸ Panels no longer lists Presets, Versions, History, Info, Keywords and Actions, which replaced the right panel without saying so.
- AI model downloads and setup are on one page, Settings ▸ AI Models, which also lets you choose where the SAM 3 model is kept. A missing model opens that page.
- In the Modern layout, a button at the top right opens a second bar with delete, flag, rating and Info.
- Detect Faces runs in the background and no longer freezes the window; Library ▸ Clear Faces and Rescan starts again for the selected photos.
- The face scan no longer takes patterns, paws and circuit boards for faces unless the detector is very sure.
- Export is a module at the far right of the top bar: it shows the photos being exported, with every export setting in a right column you can drag wider or narrower. On touch layouts Export still opens the dialog.
