### Plug-ins can make virtual copies
- With your permission, a plug-in can make a virtual copy of a photo, stacked with it, and crop the copy on its own: split a half-frame scan into its two exposures, say, the original cropped to one and a copy to the other. Undo removes a copy like one you made.
- The Plug-in Manager asks for it on install as "Make virtual copies of photos".
