### Browsed raws show their camera body's profile
- Raws you browse in the Import tab render with their camera body's own camera profile (or the profile the Import panel chooses for the body), as importing them will apply it. Nothing is written to the photos or their sidecars, and they don't count as edited.
- Setting or changing a body's profile shows on the folder's photos at once, including ones browsed before.
- Raws browsed before the app kept serial numbers are known by the serial number in their file, and a renamed file's leading number (such as `10-…`) is no longer taken for a camera body.
