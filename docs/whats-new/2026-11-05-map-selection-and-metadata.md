### Map
- Selecting a photo in the filmstrip now brings its pin onto the map when it lies off screen, panning without changing the zoom; a pin already in view never moves the map. Double-clicking a thumbnail frames its pin and zooms in on it.
- The Map panel has a Metadata section for the photos at the selected pin: GPS coordinates, Sublocation, City, State / Province and Country, plus Title, Headline, Caption and Keywords — the same editable fields as the Metadata panel, applying to every selected photo at once.
