### Generative Fill with your own account
- Remove ▸ Generative replaces a painted area using an online image service (OpenAI, Stability AI, Black Forest Labs or fal.ai) with your own API key, set up in Settings ▸ Generative Fill. Nothing is sent until you click Generate; the first time, a dialog shows the crop that will be uploaded and the cost.
- The result is kept with the library as pixels of your photo, matched to its colour and grain, and is never deleted: Retry fetches what the service already made, and earlier results can be restored.
- Ported from @OneSlips' fork.
