### Culling and browsing
- Caps Lock turns Auto Advance on while it is on: every pick, reject, rating and colour label moves to the next photo. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Photo ▸ Delete Rejected Photos removes every rejected photo in the view in one step, to Recently Deleted; Undo brings them back and the files stay on disk. Thanks @OneSlips.
- In the grid, the thumbnail of the photo a slider is editing follows the drag live. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Toggling a panel off with its key (I for Info, M for Masking…) brings back exactly what the right side showed before, closed included. Thanks @OneSlips.
- With automatic XMP writing on, photos you only browse in a folder get no sidecars until you import them, so looking at a card or an archive changes nothing in it. Thanks @OneSlips.
