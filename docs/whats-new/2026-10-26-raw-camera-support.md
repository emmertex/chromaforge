### More raws open and match their camera
- Lossy JPEG XL monochrome DNGs (Lightroom's merged monochrome LinearRaw files) decode instead of being refused.
- Canon CR3 and CR2 camera previews are read in the colour space the maker note records, so fitted camera colour starts from the right JPEG.
- Fujifilm X-S20 raws use their embedded lens corrections and spectral matrices.
- The Sigma 10-18 mm F2.8 DC DN on the Sony ILCE-6700 gets its measured distortion correction at the wide end.
