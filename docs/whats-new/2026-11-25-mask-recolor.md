### Recolor

- **Recolor** on a mask: the masked area's main colour takes a colour you pick (swatches, a hex value or an eyedropper on the photo) while keeping its own folds, gradients and highlights, so red boots become purple boots with the same light on them. Other colours inside the mask (a logo, dust, a white lining) stay; **Range** and **Color to Replace** choose which colour changes. A black shirt can become white with its folds in place.
- Works with every mask, including Describe and the AI selections. Saved with the mask and carried by Sync, Copy / Paste and effect presets.
