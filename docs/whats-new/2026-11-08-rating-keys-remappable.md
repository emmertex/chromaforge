### Rating and label keys can be remapped
- The rating keys (0 to 5) and the colour label keys (6 to 9) are commands now (Rate ★★★, Red Label and so on), so Settings ▸ Shortcuts can move them like any other key. Shift with a digit still applies and advances.
- On French AZERTY keyboards the 4 key (which types `'`) rates without Shift.
