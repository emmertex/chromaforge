### Settings is a window with one row of tabs
- In the Classic layout, Settings (Ctrl+,) opens as a window over the app with one row of tabs: General, Import, Folders, Performance, Display, Interface, Slideshow, Faces, Profiles, AI Denoise, AI Models, Generative Fill, Shortcuts, Controls, Connections and Sync. Each tab is one page of settings; tabs that don't fit the window's width are under More at the end of the row.
- The window keeps its size and place whatever the tab: a long page scrolls inside it. Drag its edges to resize it; it opens again at that size and on the tab shown last.
- Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab (or Ctrl+Page Down and Ctrl+Page Up) go to the next and previous tab, Left and Right move between tabs that have the keyboard focus, and Esc or Close closes Settings.
- The Modern layout and narrow windows keep the full-window Settings with its list of sections.
