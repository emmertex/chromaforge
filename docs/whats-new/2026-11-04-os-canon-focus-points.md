### Canon focus points
- View ▸ Show Focus Point now draws where Canon cameras focused, read from CR3, CR2 and Canon JPEG files: the
  points in focus, else the point selected. Toggle Zoom (Z) zooms to it, and the Info panel names the AF area mode
  (Face + Tracking, Zone AF, Flexizone Single…).
- Canon photos already in the library get their focus points in the background, like Sony photos. Thanks @OneSlips.
