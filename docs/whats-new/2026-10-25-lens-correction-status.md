### Lens Corrections say what they apply, and you can pick the lens
- With Enable Lens Corrections on, Develop ▸ Lens Corrections names the lens profile in use, or says the correction comes from the file itself (issue #307).
- Make and Model menus pick a lens from the lens database for a raw whose lens isn't recognised, such as a manual or adapted lens; Auto goes back to the automatic choice. A photo that records no focal length gets a Focal length field.
