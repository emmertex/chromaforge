### Saved prints from Lightroom catalogs

- Importing a Lightroom Classic catalog now brings its saved prints across as Saved Prints: the page size and margins, the layout style, every Custom Package cell where Lightroom placed it with the photo in it, image settings, page options and the Print Job settings. Open one from Print ▸ Saved Prints or by double-clicking it in Collections.
- A catalog imported before keeps its albums: importing it again turns each saved print's plain album into a Saved Print, once; a layout you changed since stays as you left it.
- What has no equivalent (identity plate, watermark, a package's photo border, a printer profile) is listed in the import result.
