### Mask thumbnails and the Roles window
- Each mask in the Masking panel, and each of its parts, shows a small thumbnail of what it selects (white on black),
  so you can tell masks apart at a glance. Resting the pointer on a mask's pin on the photo shows its overlay, as
  its row does.
- **Roles…** beside Set Role… under a People mask opens the Roles window: who has which role and where, with Clear
  for each. Thanks @OneSlips.
