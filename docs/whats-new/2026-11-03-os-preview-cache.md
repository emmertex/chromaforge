### Previews that stay
- The preview cache's new Automatic size (Settings ▸ Performance) takes 15 % of the drive's free space, at least
  20 GB and room for every photo, so a whole shoot's previews are no longer pruned while you work. Thanks @OneSlips.
- A photo whose preview is already built shows it in the loupe instead of rendering it again. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Canon CR3 thumbnails decode the camera's small preview copy, and grid thumbnails from camera previews are kept, so
  a folder you have seen before fills in at once. Thanks @OneSlips.
