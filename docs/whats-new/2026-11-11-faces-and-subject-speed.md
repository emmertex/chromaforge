### Faster masks with the Subject model, and look-alike faces at once

- Sky, landscape and depth masks no longer wait for the Subject model: it now runs only for Subject, Background, People and Object masks, so a sky mask is as quick with the model installed as without it.
- People: right-click an unnamed face to select all its look-alikes at once (then name or ignore them together), or to ignore just that face.
- Settings ▸ Faces shows where face detection runs (GPU or CPU) as soon as the tab opens, instead of "Not loaded yet".
