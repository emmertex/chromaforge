### Control surfaces: MX Creative Console and Loupedeck+
- Turn dials and press keys on a Logitech MX Creative Console (Keypad, Dialpad, Actions Ring) or a Loupedeck+ to
  edit: every develop slider, mask and brush settings, ratings, flags, presets and the tools follow the hardware.
  Pages follow what you are doing (Crop, Masking, Brush…), a fast turn speeds up, and one turn is one undo step.
- Settings → Controls draws the device: click a key or dial (or touch it, *Touch to find*) to set what it does,
  drag actions onto it, switch pages and profiles (Default, Wedding, Portrait, Fashion, Landscape, Real Estate), and
  tune the dial feel. Imports TactileBridge settings, Logi profiles (.lp5) and Loupedeck+ profiles.
- An on-screen overlay names each change and its new value (Settings → Interface → On-Screen Overlay); keys can
  apply a preset by name; destructive keys need a hold.
- The console talks to the app through a plugin for Logi Options+ that pairs once over a local port; Settings →
  Controls installs it when a plugin package comes with the app. Ported from @OneSlips' fork (TactileBridge).
