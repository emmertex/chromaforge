### Albums are now Collections
- The Library calls albums Collections, as Lightroom Classic does: Collections in the sidebar, New Collection…,
  New Smart Collection…, the target collection (B), the import review's Collection field, smart collection rules,
  messages and Help, in every language. Your albums are all still there; nothing in your library changes, and an
  older version of the app opens it as before. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Scripts and agents can use `collection.*` (and `collections.list`) as well as the `album.*` commands they used
  before.
