### Settle duplicate groups one at a time
- In Find Duplicates, each group has its own buttons: Remove N and Keep the Rest sends that group's marked photos to Recently Deleted and takes the group off the list, and Keep All keeps the whole group. A long list can now be worked through a few groups at a time (issue #1399).
- ↑ Apply This and All Above settles every group from the first down to that one in one step, as in the faces review.
