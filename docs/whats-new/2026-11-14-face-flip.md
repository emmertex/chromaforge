### Face suggestions match the same person more often
- Each face is now read both as it is and mirrored, and the two are averaged before it is compared with the faces you have named. On the LFW benchmark with AuraFace, more of a person's photos reach the match bar, especially small faces in group photos, with no more strangers matched. Recognising a face takes about twice as long, and faces already scanned are scanned once more, in the background. Settings ▸ Faces ▸ Matching turns it off.
