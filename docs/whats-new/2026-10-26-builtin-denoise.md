### AI Denoise works out of the box

- AI Denoise no longer needs a model: a built-in denoiser (block matching and collaborative
  filtering, after a noise level measured from each photo's own colour planes) cleans Bayer raws
  with nothing to install, on the graphics card when there is one that agrees with the processor.
- A downloaded or installed model is still offered in Settings ▸ AI Denoise and is used instead
  when you choose it.
