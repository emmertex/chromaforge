### Finding faces uses the GPU

- Finding faces (Library ▸ Find Faces Again, the background scan, and faces in Retouch and People masks) now runs the face detector and the face landmarks on the graphics card when a quick check shows it gives the same results faster. Finding faces across a year or a folder of photos is several times quicker on most computers.
- Settings ▸ Faces shows the graphics card detection runs on; "Use the CPU only" keeps it on the processor, as before.
