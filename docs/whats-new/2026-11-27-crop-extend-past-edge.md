### Extend a crop past the photo's edge
- Crop has a new **Extend Past the Edge** checkbox: the crop box can reach past the photo, up to half its size on each side. The area past the photo shows as grey canvas.
- **Fill Edges** fills it: **Content-Aware** from the photo itself on your computer, or **Generative** with the generative fill service and key you set up in Settings → Generative Fill. **Clear Fill** removes it. Each is one undo step, and the fill follows later edits.
