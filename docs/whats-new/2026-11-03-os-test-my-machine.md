### Test my machine
- Settings ▸ Performance ▸ Exports: choose how many photos export side by side and whether exports use the GPU, or
  click Test my machine: it exports a few of your photos at several settings for a few minutes and keeps the
  fastest, using the graphics card and the processor together when that is faster. Thanks @OneSlips.
- When the graphics card stops responding or is restarted, the app says so. Thanks @OneSlips.
