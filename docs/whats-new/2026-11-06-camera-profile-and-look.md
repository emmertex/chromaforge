### A camera profile and a look are two layers
- A camera profile with a look of its own (a tone curve or look table) develops with that look under the default look ("As profile"). Any other look sits on the profile's colour only, so two looks are never stacked.
- The profile row says which camera profile the look sits on ("on …"), and the profile menu has a Camera Profiles submenu.
- Settings ▸ Profiles lists the imported camera profiles, with the photos that use each, and can delete one. Its photos go back to the camera default.
- Profile groups can be made, moved and filled from commands. A camera body can adopt, release or clear its own camera profile.
- Photos edited before keep exactly the look they had.
