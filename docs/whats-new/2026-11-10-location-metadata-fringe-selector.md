### Delete All Location Metadata and the Fringe Color Selector

- Map > Photo > Delete All Location Metadata clears the GPS position and the place fields (sublocation, city, state, country, country code) of the selected photos in one undo step.
- Develop > Tools > Fringe Color Selector is an eyedropper: click a colour fringe and Defringe is pointed at its purple or green hue range, with an amount if it was off.
