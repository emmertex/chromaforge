### Masking panel ergonomics and overlay menu

- The masking panel has a dedicated overlay options menu (`…`) offering all overlay modes (including Image on B&W), quick toggles for automatically toggling the overlay and showing unselected mask pins, and color settings with custom swatches and opacity.
- The active mask's title and core action buttons are centered, action buttons provide larger click targets, and the redundant Edge slider has been removed for object and prompt masks in favor of Refine Edges.
