### Camera profiles from a SpyderCHECKR 24

- Create Camera Profile now also fits a profile from a Datacolor SpyderCHECKR 24 photo, as well as a ColorChecker 24: the chart is found and named either way.
- The light on the chart is evened out before fitting when that helps: falloff across the chart and glare on its patches are divided out, kept only when the grey patches end up more even (a checkbox in the dialog turns it off).
