### Both graphics cards share exports and previews
- With a second graphics card (Settings ▸ Performance), exports and Build Previews now give photos to both cards, each in proportion to its speed, instead of leaving the card for editing idle.
- The card for editing stays with you: it takes batch photos only while you aren't touching the app, one at a time, and pauses one the moment you move a slider, brush or pan.
- 1:1 previews build two photos per card at once, and the Automatic choice for the second card names the card it uses.
