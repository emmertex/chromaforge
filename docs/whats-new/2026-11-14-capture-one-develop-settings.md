### Capture One develop settings
- Importing a Capture One catalog now brings each photo's exposure, crop, rotation and straighten angle, flips and white balance with it. White balance is an approximation, and the result window marks it as such for raws the app edits on its relative white balance scale.
- Every other Capture One setting your photos use (Brightness, Levels, LCC, local adjustment layers, …) is listed in the result window with how many photos use it; those photos keep the rest of their settings.
- Imported photos are dated at their last change in Capture One, where the catalog records it.
