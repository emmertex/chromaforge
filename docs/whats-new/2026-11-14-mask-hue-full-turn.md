### Mask Hue can reach ±180°
- A mask's Hue slider is now an angle in degrees. Settings ▸ Interface ▸ Expanded sliders (off by default) lets it turn colours up to 180° either way instead of about 34°.
- Expanded values are not XMP compliant: Lightroom gets them clamped, and {app} keeps the whole angle in its own field.
- Masks you already edited look exactly as before.
