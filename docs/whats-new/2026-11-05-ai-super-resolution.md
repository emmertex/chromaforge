### AI Super Resolution and AI Sharpen
- With an AI upscaler installed (Real-ESRGAN General v3, an optional download, or your own), Photo ▸ Enhance… also offers AI Super Resolution, twice the width and height filled in by the model, and AI Sharpen, finer detail at the same size.
- Each makes a new 16-bit TIFF next to the photo, stacked with it; the work shows in the activity stack and can be cancelled. The classical Raw Details and Super Resolution stay as they were.
