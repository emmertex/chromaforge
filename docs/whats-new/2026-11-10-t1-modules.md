### Map, Book, Slideshow and Print menu items that were "Coming soon"

- Map: Delete GPS Coordinates, Search… (Ctrl/Cmd+F focuses the side panel's search box), Lock Markers (pins can't be dragged) and Show Saved Location Overlay (hide or show the saved locations' circles) now work from the menus.
- Book: Previous Page and Next Page, Show Guides, and Update Metadata Based Captions work from the menus; the page buttons stop at the first and last page.
- Slideshow and Print: Show Guides, and Print's Show Rulers, are checked menu items. Print's Show Guides switches off the margin, cell, bleed and dimension guides in one go.
