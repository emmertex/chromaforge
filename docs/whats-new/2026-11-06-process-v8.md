### Process version 8: smooth gradients, highlights that survive Exposure, cleaner colour noise reduction
- Raising Exposure on a raw keeps the brightest highlights the camera recorded instead of turning them white: the tone curve now rolls off into white gradually.
- Skies, vignettes and other smooth gradients no longer break into visible bands in the view and in 8-bit exports: process version 8 dithers 8-bit output with a fine ordered pattern. Black and white stay exact.
- Colour noise reduction removes colour blotches and specks in high-ISO photos more thoroughly at the default Color 25, while colour edges, thin coloured lines and stars keep their colour, and a zoomed-in view is filtered like the export.
- New photos and Reset use it. Photos edited earlier keep their look until you choose Photo ▸ Develop Settings ▸ Update to Current Process.
