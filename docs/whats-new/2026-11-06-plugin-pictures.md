### Pixel filters can bring their own icon and header
- A filter plug-in's manifest may carry an `icon` (a square SVG, shown in the Plug-in Manager in place of the icon drawn from its id) and a `header` (an SVG for the dark and light themes, shown at the top of its controls in Develop ▸ Plug-in Filters in place of its name). Both are drawn as untrusted documents: their own shapes only.
