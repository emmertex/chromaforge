### Each drive finds its own number of readers
- When an import or Reading originals reads several drives at once, each drive now has its own readers instead of sharing one count: a hard drive no longer takes readers an SSD beside it could use.
- Each drive starts with as many readers as before and adjusts: more while more readers read it faster, fewer when they slow it down. What it learned is kept until it has had no files for 10 minutes.
