### A drive that hangs during an import copy
- During Copy or Move, a card or destination drive that stops answering no longer holds up the import: copies elsewhere go on, and its files are reported as "volume not responding" (or "outcome unknown" for a copy that was under way; the original is always kept).
- Cancel returns even while a drive hangs; a raw waiting for Copy as DNG when you cancel is kept as its raw copy.
