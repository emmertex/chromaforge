### Lens Blur
- A new **Lens Blur** section in the Edit panel, after Effects: Apply, Blur Amount, Boost (brighter bokeh highlights) and Transition (how gradually the blur grows away from what is in focus). Ported from upstream.
- **In Focus** keeps the photo's subject sharp, or the selection of any mask. **Find Subject** selects people and animals with AI (SAM 3, optional download), starting where the camera focused when the file records it; the result is a normal Object mask that can be refined in Masking.
- **Shape**: Auto, Round, Horizontal or Vertical. **Move & Resize** drags the focus area on the photo.
- **Brush Refinement**: Focus and Blur brushes painted on the photo, with Amount, Size, Feather, Flow and Auto Mask.
- Every mask has a **Lens Blur** block: blur the selection or what it leaves out, with its own Amount, Boost and Transition.
- The blur is estimated from the picture, not from depth: there is no depth map yet.
