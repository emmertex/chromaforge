### Lens Blur
- A new **Lens Blur** section in the Edit panel blurs the background (or foreground) after the fact, from the photo's estimated depth.
- Pick what stays sharp with the **Focus Picker** (click a point or drag an area), or set the focal distance and range by hand.
- Choose the shape of blurred highlights: circle, five or six aperture blades, or a mirror-lens ring.
- With the depth model installed (AI Models) the blur follows a real depth map; without it the depth is estimated from the subject, and the panel says so.
