### Videos without a date tag keep their capture date
- MP4 clips that record their date only in the movie header (common on phones) are now dated by it, in your time zone so they sort among the photos taken with them, instead of landing under Unknown Date (issue #672).
- A clip with no date at all takes its file's date, as photos do.
- Nikon clips take the date and time zone the camera recorded, so they line up with the photos from the same body.
