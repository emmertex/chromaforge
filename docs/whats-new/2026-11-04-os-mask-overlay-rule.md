### Judge masks while you paint them
- While you brush, drag a gradient's handle or draw a colour or tone area on a mask that already has settings, no
  overlay covers the photo, not even one switched on with O, so you see the effect as you paint. Hovering a mask's
  row or pin shows no overlay meanwhile. Thanks @OneSlips.
- O now turns off every mask overlay, Show All Overlays included. While all masks show, a pill on the photo says so;
  click it to hide them. Show All Overlays is no longer kept between sessions.
