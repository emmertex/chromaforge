### Calibration saturation, White Balance readout, brush overlay
- Calibration's primary Saturation sliders change saturation instead of turning colours in hue like the Hue sliders (process version 9; Update to Current Process brings older edits over) (issue #1031).
- The White Balance Selector's loupe shows the R / G / B values of the spot under it right below the loupe (issue #1094).
- After a brush stroke the mask's overlay stays on show so the painted area can be judged; moving a slider hides it again (issue #1042).
