### Masks stay on the photo, fixes
- Old gradients keep their region when they move onto the photo under a strong lens or perspective correction, and a linear gradient's guide lines are drawn along the warp instead of straight.
- Masks pasted, synced or loaded with a preset land where they were drawn, and masks turn with a rotation brought in by Sync or Paste.
- An Object click on an old mask outside the photo is refused, and brush points a strong warp cannot place no longer lay stray dabs.
