### Cleaner Masking panel
- Mask and component preview thumbnails are larger and easier to read, and unselected rows in the mask tree no longer carry heavy background boxes (issue #1111).
- The **Who** and **Role** controls under a selected People mask component are hidden by default; toggle them from the component's `…` or right-click menu (**Show Who & Roles**).
- In **Create New Mask ▸ People**, body parts now appear as a vertical list of checkboxes.
