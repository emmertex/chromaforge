### Import Settings: a wider panel and preset lists that show whole names
- The Import tab's settings column resizes by its left edge, like the side panels, and keeps its width.
- The Add a preset list is as wide as its longest preset or folder name (within the screen), so long names are no longer cut to a few letters; it still searches, folds folders and draws only the rows in view, even with 10,000 presets.
- The camera profile menus take the column's width, and their lists show whole profile names.
- A Manage Presets… button beside the preset stack opens the Presets Manager.
- The Import tab shows the Library toolbar (flags, rating, colour labels, sort, thumbnail size) and the filter bar when they are on, and each photo shows its shutter speed, aperture, ISO and focal length in a strip at its top.
