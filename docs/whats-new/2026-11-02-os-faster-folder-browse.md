### Faster folder browsing and exports
- Browsing a folder in Local shows its first photos within about a second: the files are read in the order the view shows them, while the rest follow in the background. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Exports render more photos side by side on machines with many cores (half the cores, up to 12). Thanks @OneSlips.
- The memory for caches grows with the computer: a quarter of its memory, up to 16 GB (it stopped at 1.5 GB), so a big machine no longer re-renders every photo it goes back to. Thanks @OneSlips.
