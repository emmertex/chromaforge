### Drafts straight from the GPU, and two graphics cards
- While you drag a slider, the Detail view's drafts are drawn straight from the graphics card: the
  image no longer travels back to the processor and up again for every update.
- Settings ▸ Performance ▸ Graphics card for editing picks the card the Develop views render on
  (Automatic: the one your screen is connected to). A card that is no longer there falls back to
  Automatic and says so.
- Settings ▸ Performance ▸ Second graphics card: with two cards, exports, previews built ahead and
  thumbnails also render on the other one, so the editing card stays free for your edits. If it
  stops responding, the editing card carries on. Thanks @OneSlips.
