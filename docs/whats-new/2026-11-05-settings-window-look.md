### Settings shows every tab at once
- The Classic Settings window opens tall and wide enough for its whole row of tabs, so no tab hides under More until you make the window narrower. It still fits the screen: on a smaller screen it opens at the screen's height.
- Each tab reads as one plain page: a bold header over a thin line for each section, the setting's name in a column on the left, its checkbox, buttons or choices beside it, and a short grey explanation under it. Choices are small buttons side by side, the chosen one filled.
- The window still opens at the size you leave it at. Based on work by @OneSlips.
