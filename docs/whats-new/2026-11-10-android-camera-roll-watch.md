### Android: the camera roll watches for new photos
- Adding your camera roll (on the first-start question, or later from Settings ▸ Folders) now keeps an eye on it: the app lists it again when you open the app, when it comes back to the front and every few minutes, and photos the library doesn't have yet are added on their own, without the import review. A toast tells you how many new photos arrived; nothing found says nothing.
- The camera roll's row in Settings ▸ Folders has a *Watch for new photos* checkbox to turn the watching off.
