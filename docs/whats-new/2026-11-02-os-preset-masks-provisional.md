### Presets add masks as Lightroom Classic does
- Applying a preset with masks adds them to the photo's own. Applying the same preset again right away replaces the
  masks it just added instead of doubling them; after another preset or an edit of those masks, they are yours and
  the preset adds a fresh set. Masks with a placeholder name are named after the preset (and what they select when
  it brings several). Object and Describe selections are made again on each photo. Thanks @OneSlips.
