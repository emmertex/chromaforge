### Print: cell sizes in centimetres, typed and dragged
- The Print module's Guides panel has a Units choice (in, cm, mm) for the rulers, the cell dimensions and every size field.
- Picture Package lists its cells with their sizes, which you can type and remove one by one; both packages add a cell of any typed size.
- In Custom Package, click a cell to select it: drag its corner handle to resize it (it snaps to the margins and other cells), or type its width, height and position.
