### The Masks list as a tree
- A mask's parts hang under it as branches of a tree, each with + (add), − (subtract) or ∩ (intersect) in a column before its thumbnail, and its own Invert button.
- Only the selected mask's parts show, so a preset's many masks fit one row each; the arrow on a mask's row opens or folds its parts.
- Parts are named after what they select ("Groom - Clothes, Hair", "Wedding dress", "Water"), as masks are, and clearing a mask's name gives it back its automatic one.
- The eye stays on every mask's row: it switches that mask's effect on and off for a quick before / after. O still shows the overlay.
