### Measure colour against another editor
- The command-line tool's new `compare` measures how far one render of a photo is from another, from any editor or
  camera: it lines the two up and reports the colour difference (CIEDE2000) by tone and hue, how much lighter, more
  saturated or hue-shifted one is, and can write a colour-coded difference map. Useful when moving your photos from
  another editor, or checking a camera calibration.
