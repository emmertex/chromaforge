### Template folders in Print and Slideshow
- Print ▸ New Template Folder and Slideshow ▸ New Template Folder make a folder in the Template Browser. Your own templates are grouped under "User Templates" and one group per folder; empty folders are kept.
- Right-click a template to move it to a folder, and right-click a folder to rename it or delete it (its templates move back to "User Templates").
- The Print dialog's template list has the same folders: pick one before Save or Import, and move a template from the list.
