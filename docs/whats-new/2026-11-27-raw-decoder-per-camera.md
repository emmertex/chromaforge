### Choose the raw decoder per camera

- **Settings ▸ Import ▸ Raw decoder** sets, for each camera in the library, which decoder reads its raw files: **Auto**, the **built-in** decoder (lcc-raw) or **LibRaw**. The picture is always demosaiced and developed by the app.
- **Auto** keeps the built-in decoder and hands LibRaw only the files it can't read, which used to open as their embedded preview.
- LibRaw comes with the desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux. The web and Android versions don't have it, and show the choice greyed out.
