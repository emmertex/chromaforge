### Camera profiles from a color chart

- **Photo ▸ Create Camera Profile from Color Chart…:** photograph a ColorChecker 24, and the app finds it, fits a camera profile (DCP) for your camera and adds it to your camera profiles. Choose the light (Daylight / Flash or Tungsten) and the look (Photo, or Repro for artwork); add a second chart photo under the other light for a dual-illuminant profile. The dialog shows how closely each patch is matched and remembers your lights and look. Thanks @OneSlips.
- **Profile Manager:** Settings ▸ Profiles now renames profiles, moves them into your own groups, reorders them, removes built-in looks (and restores them), and does the same for a whole group. Thanks @OneSlips.
- **Import:** the Import dialog can give every raw one camera profile for this import. Thanks @OneSlips.
