### Presets, fixes
- A colour preset applied after a black and white one turns the photo back to colour: every built-in colour preset now sets the colour treatment.
- A preset changes only the settings it names. Your other edits stay as they are, also in a panel the preset styles, and the preview you see while hovering over a preset matches what a click applies.
- Below 100 %, a preset's amount is measured from the look your photo had when it was imported with a default preset, so 50 % looks the same however you edited the photo before; at 0 % the settings the preset names go back to that import look. Photos imported without a default preset are measured from their current settings, as before.
