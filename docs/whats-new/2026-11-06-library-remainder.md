### Library and import touches
- While you drag a slider with several photos selected, every selected photo's grid thumbnail follows the drag, not only the one you're editing.
- Settings ▸ Performance offers memory sizes up to what your computer allows, not just up to 4 GB, and Automatic shows the size it picks.
- Quitting no longer asks to back up a catalog that hasn't changed since its last backup.
- An import whose previews, AI masks or Smooth Skin were still being prepared when the app quit or crashed picks up where it stopped the next time you open the library. Stopping it yourself means it stays stopped.
