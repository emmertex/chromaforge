### Presets Manager and At Import
- Photo ▸ Develop Settings ▸ Manage Presets… (also on a preset's right-click menu) lists every preset by group, built-in ones too, with a search. Star a preset, mark it At Import, hide it from the Presets panel and the pickers, or delete one of your own. Based on work by @OneSlips.
- Presets marked At Import get a group of their own in the Presets panel and come first, after Favorites, wherever you pick a preset (import, Quick Develop, Settings, tethering). Every preset picker can now search.
