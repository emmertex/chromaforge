### Pause, clear and restart face and search indexing
- Recognise faces in Settings ▸ Faces is now off until you switch it on, also after downloading a model: scanning a big library can take a long time. If you had switched it on, it stays on.
- Settings ▸ Faces and Settings ▸ AI Models ▸ Semantic search show how far the face scan and search indexing are, with Pause, Resume, Clear… and Restart…. Clear and Restart ask first.
- While the face scan or search indexing runs, its row in the activity stack has a pause button (resume while paused, also after restarting the app) and a gear that opens its settings.
- Semantic search can be switched off in Settings ▸ AI Models: nothing is indexed and the AI button leaves the search box.
