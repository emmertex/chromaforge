### Colour and Luminance Range pickers
- Color Range: a click on the photo picks the colour, ⇧-click adds one (up to five), ⌥-click removes the nearest pin, and a drag over an area picks up to three of its main colours. Each picked place shows a pin you can drag to pick there again; the panel shows the colours as swatches.
- Luminance Range has a Pick button: a click selects the tones round the one there, a drag the tones of the area, ⇧ widens the range. Ported from OneSlips' fork.
