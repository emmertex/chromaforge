### People and remembered objects in Create New Mask
- Create New Mask has People and Remembered tiles. People opens a picker under the tiles: Everyone or one of the faces found on the photo (by name when it has one, so a preset made from the mask finds them on other photos) and the parts to select. Remembered selects an object you taught the app on this photo. Based on work by @OneSlips.
- A mask's menu has Remember as…: name what the mask selects ("Wedding dress") and find it again on other photos.
- One person of a large group (a team or class photo) now gets a mask of their own size: each small person is segmented on their own crop, and their silhouette fills in where the whole photo is too coarse.
- A People mask that finds nobody on a photo selects nothing, instead of guessing at the middle of the frame.
- Finding people again by their look works on group photos too, and `people.recognize` / `people.cluster` name or group faces by look.
- The automatic re-match of People masks by name or role, when a photo's looks arrive, no longer adds an undo step.
- In the Compact layout the Who row under a People mask fits the panel: Set Role… and Roles… wrap to a second line.
