### Presets Manager for large collections
- The Presets Manager opens at a size that fits your screen, keeps the size you drag it to and never grows past the screen.
- Presets and groups are a table with the actions in columns: At Import, Rename, Group, Hide and Delete. Double-click a name to rename it.
- Ctrl-click or Shift-click picks several presets; drag them onto a group, or onto any preset of a group, to move them there.
- Deleting a group asks at the group, and can move its presets to another group first.
- Collections of 10 000 presets and more scroll smoothly.
