### Color Mixer inside a mask
- A mask can carry its own Color Mixer: the eight colours' Hue, Saturation and Luminance, changed only inside the
  mask and weighted by it, on the CPU and the GPU alike. Open **Color Mixer** under the mask's Color group: pick
  Hue, Saturation or Luminance, move a colour's slider, or drag up and down on the photo with the targeted
  adjustment. Agents: `mask.mixer`, `mask.mixerTargeted`. Thanks @OneSlips.
