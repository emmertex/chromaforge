### Refine Photos and Find Previous Process Photos

- Library ▸ Refine Photos… (Ctrl+Alt+R) is a culling pass over the photos in view: picks become unflagged and unflagged photos become rejected, rejected ones stay. It asks first, saying how many photos each step changes, and is one undo step.
- Library ▸ Find Previous Process Photos shows the photos that still render with an older process version. Smart Collections and the filter bar's rules have a new Process Version field to build the same search.
