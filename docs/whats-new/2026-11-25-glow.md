### Glow
- A new **Glow** group in the Effects panel spreads the bright parts of the picture over it as a soft halo, with the controls DaVinci Resolve's Glow effect has: **Glow** for the strength, **Threshold** for how bright a part must be to glow, **Radius** for the halo's width, **H/V Ratio** to stretch it one way, **Gain** and **Gamma** to shape its brightness and falloff, a **Screen** (gentler) or **Add** (hotter) composite, and **Recovery** to bring the bright regions' detail back through the glow.
- The halo is measured against the frame, so a preview and an export match, and a zoomed view shows halos reaching in from outside it.
