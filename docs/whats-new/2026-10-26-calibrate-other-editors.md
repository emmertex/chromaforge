### Camera calibrations for Capture One, darktable and more
- `calibrate-lightroom fit --editor capture-one` (or `darktable`, `rawtherapee`, `dxo-photolab`) fits a camera's
  colour to another editor's exports of your raws, so you can open them in {app} with that editor's look. Its looks
  sit beside Lightroom's in Profile ▸ Camera profile and never replace the camera's default.
