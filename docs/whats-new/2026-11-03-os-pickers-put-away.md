### Pickers put themselves away
- A targeted adjustment or Point Color picker is put away when you choose another Color Mixer tab or group, mixer
  mode, curve channel or panel, touch a slider, or press Esc, and it no longer follows into Masks. Its button shows a
  picker with an up/down arrow.
- In Masks, a click on the photo with no tool armed zooms in, as in Edit. Ported from @OneSlips' work.
