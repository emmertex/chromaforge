### Draw face regions by hand
- Photo ▸ People ▸ Draw Face Regions turns on a drawing mode in the loupe: drag a box around a face it missed, and when you let go it becomes a face region with its name box open. Esc or the menu item again leaves the mode.
- The new `photo.addRegion` command adds a face region from a box in the photo's own frame; it is undoable and, like the other region edits, does not rewrite the sidecar.
