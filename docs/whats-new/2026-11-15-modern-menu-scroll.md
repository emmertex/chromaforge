### The Modern menu bar scrolls under a finger again
- With the ☰ menu bar open, a sideways swipe scrolls the bar (and the Develop dock) on a touch screen instead of being handed to the title under it, and the open menu no longer pulls the bar back to its own title.
- The loupe's shot-info strip keeps clear of the floating dock in the Modern layout (it was clipped behind it), and hides while a group's card is open above the dock.
