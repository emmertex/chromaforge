### Map side panel metadata editing
- The Map module's side panel now displays and edits location metadata (GPS coordinates, Sublocation, City, State/Province, Country, and ISO Country Code) and IPTC details (Title, Headline, Caption, and Keywords) for selected photos (issue #784).
- The catalog now stores ISO country codes (`country_code`), preserved across reverse geocoding, XMP sidecar sync, metadata presets, and export.
