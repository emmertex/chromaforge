### Update AI Mask
- **Update AI Mask** computes a mask's AI selection again from this photo: in the mask's menu, a part's menu, the
  photo's right-click menu (right-click a mask's pin for that mask's menu) and Photo ▸ Develop Settings ▸ Update All
  AI Masks for the selected photos. A mask whose AI selection wasn't computed for this photo (copied, synced or
  brought by a preset) shows an amber badge on its row: click it to update.
- AI masks now compute in the background with one row in the activity stack, which ✕ stops. Undo stops what is
  still computing, and a preset that brings Object or Describe masks starts computing them by itself. Thanks
  @OneSlips.
