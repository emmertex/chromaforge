### Library: alternatives, selection, naming and counts
- Photo ▸ Show Alternatives (also on a photo's right-click menu) brings up the frames around a photo from its folder, imported or not; pick the ones to keep and import them with the same edit. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Delete Rejected Photos asks how: remove them from the library (Undo brings them back) or also move their files to the system trash. Thanks @OneSlips.
- The filter bar shows live counts beside its flags and colour labels. Thanks @OneSlips.
- A click on a photo inside a selection makes it the active one and keeps the selection; a click on the frame around a thumbnail selects that photo alone; Select All keeps up with photos that are still being imported. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Sorting by capture date interleaves two cameras through the day in collections and inside a Recently Added import too. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Rename Photos and Export offer saved naming presets, a + Number button and a field for each {text:Label} tag, typed for each use (a couple's initials). Thanks @OneSlips.
- The grid and the filmstrip have scroll bars you can see, and while the grid scrolls a label says where you are in the shoot. Thanks @OneSlips.
- Quitting with a catalog backup due asks whether to back up now or skip this time. Thanks @OneSlips.
