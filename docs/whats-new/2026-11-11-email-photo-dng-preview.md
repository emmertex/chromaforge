### Email Photo and Update DNG Preview

- File ▸ Email Photo… exports the selected photos as small JPEGs (you pick the long edge and quality) and opens a new message with them attached in your mail program: `xdg-email` on Linux, Mail on macOS, Outlook on Windows. With no mail program to ask, the files stay in a temporary folder that is shown, and the message says where they are.
- Metadata ▸ Update DNG Preview & Metadata rewrites the selected DNGs with a fresh JPEG preview of the current edit and the photo's current XMP, without touching the raw data. DNGs written by Adobe software and files it does not recognise are refused with the reason and left as they were.
