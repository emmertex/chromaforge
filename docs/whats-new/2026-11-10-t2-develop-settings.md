### Crop and process version menu items
- Develop ▸ Settings ▸ Crop to Same Aspect Ratio (Shift+A) gives every other selected photo the active photo's crop ratio, as one undo step (issue #989).
- Settings ▸ Crop As Shot (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R) puts the crop back to the full camera frame.
- Settings ▸ Process Version lists every rendering process, with the active photo's checked; choosing one puts the selected photos on it (undoable).
