### Auto Sync on the bottom bar, Show Before, Favorites and presets from an older install
- Compact layout: the bottom bar carries an Auto Sync switch at its right end in the grid and in Detail, lit while edits go to every selected photo. Its name shows on a wide window; a narrow one keeps the switch, and its tooltip says what it does.
- The `\` key is named Show Before (View ▸ Before/After): it shows the Before in place, as Set Before keeps it.
- The sidebar section of folders to browse is called Favorites, with Add Folder to Favorites, Remove from Favorites and Show Hidden Favorites.
- Presets an older install kept on this computer, under an earlier name of {app}, come into the shared presets by themselves the first time a library opens: develop presets with their favourites and hidden and At Import marks, local adjustment presets and export presets. File ▸ More Imports ▸ Import Presets from Older Installs asks again; the same preset is kept once.
