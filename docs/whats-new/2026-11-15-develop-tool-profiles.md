### Develop tool profiles
- Settings ▸ Develop: choose which groups and sliders the Develop panel shows. Make your own tool profiles, rename and delete them, switch groups off, hide single sliders and options, and gather the sliders you use most in a Custom group, in your order.
- Two built-in profiles: Default (everything, as before) and Witchcraft (Primaries and HSL Curves, without Color Grading).
- A low row at the top of the Develop panel switches profile; its gear opens Settings ▸ Develop.
- Browse Profiles moved into the row of tools with Edit, Crop and Remove. The profile row and the Edit title line are gone: Auto, B&W and HDR are now at the top of Basic.
