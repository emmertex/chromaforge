### A filmstrip you can resize, and shot info under the photo
- Drag the filmstrip's top edge to make it between 96 and 360 points high; its thumbnails grow with it. A double click puts it back to its usual height, and the height is kept after a restart.
- View ▸ Shot Info (Shift+I) shows one line under the photo in the loupe: aperture, shutter, ISO, focal length, lens and size in pixels. Zoomed in, it sits over the photo's bottom edge, and it steps aside while a tool works on the photo.
