### Running tasks show in the identity plate
- In the Classic layout, work in progress now shows where Lightroom Classic shows it: in the module bar, in the identity plate's place, instead of a card over the photos. One task shows its name, its progress bar and ✕ to stop it; several show how many are running over a thin bar each.
- Click there to list every task, with its progress, time left and ✕. Esc or a click outside closes the list. The identity plate comes back once nothing is running.
- With the module bar hidden, or Show Status and Activity off in Identity Plate Setup, tasks run on without showing. The Modern layout keeps its activity card.
