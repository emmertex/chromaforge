### Lightroom's brush
- The mask brush now paints like Lightroom Classic's: Size is the solid core, Feather adds a soft halo outside it
  (the cursor shows both circles), and overlapping soft strokes blend into one shape instead of pinching where they
  meet. Strokes you painted before keep their look.
- Lightroom brush masks in presets and sidecars come in at their real size and softness, and presets exported as XMP
  carry brush strokes.
- Auto Mask follows Lightroom's tolerances with a pixel-sharp edge, and a new Auto Mask range slider widens or
  tightens it for the next strokes.
