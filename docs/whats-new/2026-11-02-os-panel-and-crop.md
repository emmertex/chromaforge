### Tool panels and the crop tool
- Closing Crop, Remove, Masking or Red Eye puts back the panel that was showing before it (Edit, Info, …), at the same scroll position, or leaves the panel hidden if it was. Based on work by @OneSlips.
- In the Masking panel with no mask tool armed, dragging a zoomed photo where there is no pin or handle pans it, as in the loupe.
- New photos start with Constrain Crop on, so a crop stays inside the photo after a rotation or lens correction; Settings ▸ Import ▸ "Constrain crop by default" turns that off.
- In the crop tool, anywhere along an edge of the crop box resizes that side (not only its middle handle), and X on an untouched photo keeps the crop locked to the ratio it shows (3:2 becomes 2:3).
