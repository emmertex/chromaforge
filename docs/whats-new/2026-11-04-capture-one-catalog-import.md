### Import Capture One catalogs
- File ▸ More Imports ▸ Import Lightroom or Capture One Catalog… also opens a Capture One catalog (its `.cocatalogdb` file): the originals inside the catalog or referenced from elsewhere, variants (the second and later ones as virtual copies), star ratings, colour tags, IPTC fields, keywords with their hierarchy, and your albums inside their projects and groups.
- Orange and pink colour tags have no label here and come in without one; smart albums, trashed images and Capture One's own folders (Recent Imports, All Images) are left out, and the result window says so. Develop settings are not read yet.
- As with a Lightroom catalog, the import goes into the open library or a new one, is one undo step, and importing the same catalog again adds nothing twice.
