### Crop aspect ratios, fixes
- A ratio saved in the Crop panel's aspect menu can always be applied, and a Custom ratio or Save that can't be taken says why and leaves the menu open.
- A saved ratio that settings can't be written for isn't kept, and one saved ratio that doesn't read no longer resets the other presets in the settings file.
- The aspect button names the saved ratio that matches the crop exactly, and a long list of saved ratios scrolls.
