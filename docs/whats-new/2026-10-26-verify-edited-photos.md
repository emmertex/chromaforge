### Check how your edited photos come over
- The command-line tool's `calibrate-lightroom verify` renders your edited raws the way {app} imports them (from
  their XMP sidecars, or the settings Lightroom stores in its exports) and measures each against the edited export
  you made in Lightroom: the colour difference per photo, and per kind of edit (light, tone curve, colour mixer,
  white balance, crop, masks…), so you can see what carries over well before you move a whole library.
