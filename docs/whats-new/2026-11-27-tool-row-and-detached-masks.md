### Tool row buttons and a detachable mask list
- Right-click the row of tools under the histogram to hide any button but Edit, Browse Profiles and Reset included, or to move one left or right. Settings ▸ Develop ▸ Tool Row does the same.
- The mask list can float in its own window over the photo (right-click a mask, Detach Mask List, or Settings ▸ Develop). Feather, Refine Edges and the mask's sliders stay in the panel. It is off by default.
