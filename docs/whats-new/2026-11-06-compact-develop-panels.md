### Compact layout: denser Develop panels and a filled histogram
- With the Compact layout on, the Edit panel starts straight with Basic: Auto, B&W and HDR on one row, then the profile and white balance. Section headers are banded bars, and the rows inside a section (Defringe, captions such as Sharpening or Vignette, Mixer / Point Color) are quiet and line up on the same columns.
- Slider names are right-aligned next to their tracks, with short names under a caption (Amount, Radius, Hue Low…), and the right panel opens 318 points wide.
- The histogram shows the red, green and blue channels as soft filled areas that blend toward grey where they overlap, with the shot settings under it and an **Original Photo** box that shows the photo without its edit while ticked.
- The tool row under it spaces the tools evenly and ends with Reset. The Classic layout is unchanged.
