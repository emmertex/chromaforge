### Hot pixels and sensor dust, per camera
- Raw photos get their hot, dead and stuck pixels fixed while they are read, and the app remembers each camera body's pixels across photos (Detail ▸ Fix Hot and Dead Pixels turns it off for a photo).
- Remove Dust finds dust shadows, adds the dust this camera is known to have, evens shadows out by default and works on a selection in the background; its spots are outlined in amber. Settings ▸ Import can run it on new raw photos.
- Ported from @OneSlips' fork.
