### Background preparation finishes what it started

- A background build of the previews that was interrupted (you changed the folder, sort or filter, photos were imported, or the app was closed) is taken up again once the view has stayed the same for a few seconds, from the grid as well as the loupe, and after a restart.
- Stopping the build from its row in the activity list still means no: it is not started again. A library with nothing unfinished still starts nothing just because it was opened.
