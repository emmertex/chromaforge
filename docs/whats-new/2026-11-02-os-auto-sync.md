### Auto Sync
- Auto Sync is remembered app-wide: it stays as you left it after a restart and whichever library is open. Based on work by @OneSlips.
- A preset clicked in Develop's Presets panel follows Auto Sync, as a slider does: the active photo only with Auto Sync off, every selected photo with it on. Right-click a preset ▸ Apply to N Selected Photos puts it on the whole selection on purpose.
- A crop synced to a photo turned the other way (portrait and landscape) keeps how tight it is and where it sits, in that photo's own orientation, instead of becoming its whole frame.
- History steps made by one edit of several photos share a batch id (`history.list` returns `batches`).
