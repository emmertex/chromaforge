### Presets Manager: groups, renaming and Restore Built-ins
- In the Presets Manager, rename any preset in place (built-in ones too), rename, hide or delete a whole group, drag a preset onto a group to move it there, make a New Group…, import and export presets, and put the built-in presets back as shipped with Restore Built-ins. Your own groups come first. Based on work by @OneSlips.
- Preset pickers (import, Quick Develop, Settings, tethering) fold their groups, star a preset in place and show the preset you rest on as a small picture of the active photo beside the list.
- New Preset picks its group from a list of yours, with New Group… for a new one; after Update with Current Settings…, the message has an Undo button.
- Settings ▸ Interface ▸ Presets lets a library keep its own presets instead of the ones every library shares. The grid's sort now follows you from library to library, and presets are also gathered from backups kept in a separate backup folder.
