### Presets stay in the left column

- Develop's presets are always the Presets panel of the left column. They no longer take over the right side in place of the Edit panels, which could leave Develop with no sliders.
- Window > Panels > Presets shows the left column and unfolds the Presets panel; a second time folds it.
- The top bar's right-panel button and F8 bring the right side back after Tab hid it, and work in Book, Print and Slideshow too.
