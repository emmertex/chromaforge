### Library ▸ View: Lock Zoom Position, Scroll By Row, Mirror Image Mode

- Lock Zoom Position (on by default) keeps one zoom and pan for every photo; switch it off and each photo remembers its own.
- Scroll By Row makes a wheel notch scroll the grid by whole rows.
- Enable Mirror Image Mode shows the grid, loupe, compare view and filmstrip flipped left to right; photos, edits and exports are unchanged.
