### Plug-ins publish to online services

- A plug-in publish service now gets what an online photo site needs: the service's settings, each published collection's name (one remote album per collection), and each photo's title, caption and keywords.
- A plug-in service can show the site's comments in **Library ▸ Review Comments**, as Immich does.
- Flickr, SmugMug and similar sites can come as plug-ins; the app itself ships no service keys for them.
