### Clear culling marks in the Compact layout
- In the Compact layout every grid and filmstrip thumbnail has a strip under the picture with its flag, stars and colour label, large enough to read while culling and covering nothing of the photo.
- Picked and rejected photos have a soft white or red glow behind the thumbnail and a slightly lighter or darker cell; selected and active cells are plainly brighter, so a selection of picks still reads as selected.
