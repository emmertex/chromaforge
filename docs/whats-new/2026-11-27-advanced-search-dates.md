### Advanced Search finds photos by date, flag, file size and flash
- A new **Date & Time** section in Advanced Search finds photos taken, imported or edited between two dates (a year, a month or a day), and photos taken in a month or on a day of the month whatever the year, such as every 14 May (#1135).
- Item Properties gains the pick flag and file size; Photograph Information gains exposure bias and whether the flash fired.
