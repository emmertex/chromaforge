- **Names from other apps**: face regions that Lightroom, digiKam and others wrote into XMP are read and shown as boxes in the loupe (hide, resize or remove them). No AI needed.
- **People view**: View ▸ **People** (or the People button in the toolbar) lists each named person with a close-up and photo count. Click one to filter the grid; `person:` works in search.
- **Detect Faces**: Library ▸ **Detect Faces** finds faces in the selected photos with the MediaPipe Face Landmarker and adds unnamed boxes in one undo step. It offers the small one-time download the first time. Boxes from XMP or that you named are never touched. Finds faces down to about 60 pixels; tiny faces in big group photos can be missed.
- **Recognise faces (experimental)**: Settings ▸ **Faces**. With a recognition model chosen, a background scan works out what each face looks like and suggests names from faces you have already named. A suggestion is only a label such as "Jane Doe?" until you confirm it; nothing is named for you.
- **Everything stays local**: face data is cached beside the library, not in the catalog or XMP.

<NeedsImage what="People view with a few named people, each with a face close-up and photo count" />

## Needs

- Detection: the MediaPipe face model (a few MB, Apache-2.0).
- Recognition: a model you choose in Settings ▸ Faces ▸ Download after accepting its terms (AuraFace 261 MB, SFace 39 MB). Nothing is bundled, and everything else works without one.
- Faces are found from the photo upright and uncropped, so crops and edits do not matter. Raws use their embedded preview.
- Face detection and recognition run on the GPU when a background check finds it matches the CPU and is faster; Settings ▸ Faces shows the device. Otherwise they run on the CPU unless a check shows the GPU gives the same results faster. Settings ▸ Faces has **Run face models on the CPU only**.

## Limits

- Partial: no separated clusters, no manual tagging beyond naming, and regions are never written back to the sidecar.
- Detection is a replacement for the earlier detector, whose training data had a restrictive licence; an old one is listed so you can remove it.
- Not a Lightroom feature set: accuracy is unmeasured against Lightroom.
