- **Where**: Edit ▸ **Detail** ▸ **AI Denoise** (switch) and **Amount** (0 to 100). Turning it on starts at 50.
- **Works out of the box**: a built-in denoiser (block matching and collaborative filtering after measuring the photo's noise). No download and no weights. It is a classical filter, not a trained network.
- **Raw only by default**: Bayer raw files. A JPEG, PNG or TIFF is denoised only with a model for finished pictures. X-Trans, Foveon and demosaiced DNG are not supported. The panel says so on photos it cannot handle.
- **Non-destructive**: the cleaned picture is cached data, made in the background and shown in previews and exports. Your file is never changed. Exports make it if it is missing.
- **Manual noise reduction** (Luminance, Color) stays separate and works on every photo.

## Switching on many photos

- With **Auto Sync** on, every selected raw switches together (one undo step) and is made in the background in one activity row with time left; the row's cancel stops the batch.
- **Settings ▸ Import ▸ AI Denoise from** turns it on for new raws at or above an ISO (Off, 800 to 12800).
- Settings ▸ **AI Denoise**: choose Speed (Automatic, Graphics card, Processor), make the cleaned picture for the photo you are viewing, **Denoise all photos with an Amount**, and set the cache limit.

<Shot name="ai-settings-denoise" alt="Settings, AI Denoise tab: speed, photos and cache" />

## Optional models

- Settings ▸ **AI Denoise** ▸ **Install from file…** installs an ONNX model you supply, after you accept its terms. A separate slot holds a model for finished pictures.
- The desktop app can offer the upstream project's own raw model (MIT or Apache-2.0) as a download; the default build offers no other downloads. Opt-in research weights exist only in builds that enable them.
- See [Models and set-up](../models/).

## Hardware

- Runs on the GPU (wgpu) where a card agrees with the CPU result and is faster, else on the CPU. Speeds measured: one tile 22 ms on a desktop GPU against about 400 ms on one CPU thread.
- Tiles can run on another computer's graphics card (advanced; see the project documentation).

## Limits

- Partial. No quality comparison with Lightroom on real high-ISO raws yet.
- A linear-RGB path and merged HDR DNGs are not supported.
