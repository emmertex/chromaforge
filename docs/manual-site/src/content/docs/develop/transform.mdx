- **Upright**: **Off**, **Auto**, **Level**, **Vertical**, **Full**, **Guided**. Partial: tuned on synthetic scenes; a mode that finds nothing says why over the photo and suggests Guided.
- **Guided Upright**: draw guides along lines that should be straight; select a guide and `Delete` removes it.
- **Constrain Crop** fits the crop to the corrected photo.
- **Sliders**: **Vertical**, **Horizontal**, **Rotate**, **Aspect**, **Scale**, **Offset X**, **Offset Y**.
- A fine **grid** shows over the photo while a slider is dragged.
- Auto modes sync to other photos as a recipe and are analysed on each; Guided is not synced.
