- **Texture**, **Clarity**, **Dehaze**: in Basic and in the Effects dock card.
- **Vignette**: **Vignette** amount, **Midpoint**, **Roundness**, **Feather**, **Highlights**; style **Highlight**, **Color**, **Paint**. Post-crop: it follows the crop.
- **Grain**: amount, **Size**, **Size Variation**, **Roughness**, **Clumping**, **Color**, **Response**, **Seed**. From process V7, irregular particles that do not change with the picture's size. See [Process versions](../process-versions/).
- Dehaze from V3 keeps shadows from going black.
- The eye on a section header turns its adjustments off for comparison.
