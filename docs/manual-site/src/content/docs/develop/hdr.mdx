- **HDR** button next to **Auto** and **B&W** at the top of the Edit panel: edits the photo with highlights above SDR white.
- **Headroom limit**: 0 to 5 stops, marked on the HDR histogram.
- **SDR rendition**: its own Brightness, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Clarity and a Blend, for thumbnails and SDR exports. **View ▸ Loupe Info ▸ HDR Preview** shows it.
- **Visualize HDR** (`⇧H`): grey below SDR white, coloured bands for each stop above it.
- **HDR display**: on an HDR display the loupe shows above SDR white (macOS EDR, Windows HDR, Wayland compositors with colour management). Partial: SDR displays are unchanged; checked on a few machines.
- **Export**: HDR JPEG with a gain map (ISO 21496-1, Adobe and Apple XMP), 10-bit AVIF (Rec. 2020 PQ). Partial. See [Export](../../output/export/).
- **Apple gain maps**: a helper reconstructs the HDR picture from Apple's legacy gain map and its SDR base. It is not connected to import yet.
- **HDR merge** makes a 32-bit DNG: [Photo merge](../merge/).

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  **HDR** is in the **Light** card. **View ▸ Loupe Info** holds Visualize HDR and HDR Preview.
</Aside>
