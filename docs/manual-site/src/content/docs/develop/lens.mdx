- **Enable Lens Corrections** applies the profile for the lens (partial, see below).
- **Remove Chromatic Aberration** and **Defringe**: Purple and Green Amount with hue low and high. No fringe eyedropper.
- **Manual**: **Distortion**, **Vignetting** and its midpoint.
- **Save as default**: stores the Optics settings as the start for photos imported from now on, per camera.
- When nothing applies, the panel says why: no lens database, no lens in the metadata, or no profile for the lens.

## Where profiles come from

- **In the file**: DNG warp and vignette opcodes (including Pixel phones' two-stage warp).
- **Camera corrections** read from the raw: Sony ARW, Nikon NEF, Fujifilm RAF, Panasonic and Leica RW2.
- **Lens database** you supply, in lensfun format: **Edit ▸ Settings ▸ Import ▸ Lens profiles** (choose a folder, or Download). Not bundled.
- Adobe lens profiles are never used.
- **Sensor window**: crop modes are handled so profiles line up.
