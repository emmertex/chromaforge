- **Enable Lens Corrections** applies the profile for the lens (partial, see below).
- **Remove Chromatic Aberration** and **Defringe**: Purple and Green Amount with hue low and high. No fringe eyedropper.
- **Manual**: **Distortion**, **Vignetting** and its midpoint.
- **Save as default**: stores the Optics settings as the start for photos imported from now on, per camera.
- When nothing applies, the panel says why: no lens database, no lens in the metadata, or no profile for the lens.

## Where profiles come from

- **In the file**: DNG warp and vignette opcodes (including Pixel phones' two-stage warp).
- **Camera corrections** read from the raw: Sony ARW, Nikon NEF, Fujifilm RAF, Panasonic and Leica RW2.
- **Lens database** you supply, in lensfun format: **Edit ▸ Settings ▸ Import ▸ Lens profiles** (choose a folder, or Download). Not bundled.
- Adobe lens profiles are never used.
- **Sensor window**: crop modes are handled so profiles line up.

## Vignetting Calibration

<NeedsImage what="Lens Corrections with a vignetting calibration: Create, Apply, Remove, the reference line with Show Reference, and the Amount slider" />

Light fall-off measured from a reference shot, as Capture One's LCC "Uniform Light". Under **Lens Corrections**, whether or not Enable Lens Corrections is on.

- **Create**: measures the photo on screen as a reference and applies it to that photo. The photo must look like one: only an even surface, one colour, light falling off from the middle. Others are refused with the reason; a near miss (a faint dust spot) asks first, **Create Anyway**.
- **Apply**: gives the photo, or every selected photo, the reference for its camera model, lens, aperture and (zoom) focal length. Photos without one are left as they are and counted; the tooltip says which aperture is missing.
- **Amount** 0–120 % (100 % is the measured correction), and **Remove**. Part of the edit: undo, History, Sync, Copy / Paste, presets and sidecars carry it.
- **One reference per aperture.** A reference serves only photos at its own aperture, from any body of the model. A full-frame reference also serves crop modes (5:4, 1:1, 16:9, DX), not the other way round.
- **Replacing**: Create for a combination that has a reference asks first; photos that use the old one keep it.
- **On a reference photo**: the panel says so, with **Stop Using as Reference**. Create on it makes it a reference again.
- **Finding references**: the Library filter bar's Vignetting Reference picker, a smart collection's **Vignetting Reference** rule (Develop), and a small marker in the grid.
- Corrects light fall-off only: no colour cast, no dust. While it applies, the profile's vignetting is off; its distortion and CA stay. **Library ▸ Flat-Field Correction…** is a separate tool that writes corrected DNG copies.
- **Shooting a reference**: an OLED screen showing white or a diffuser over the lens (not an LCD or IPS screen); histogram peak at 2/3 to 3/4; 1/30 s or slower on a screen; focus at infinity, a tripod, VR off; the lens, focal length and aperture of the photos.
