<Shot name="develop" alt="Develop with light and colour sliders" />

- **Basic**: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks; white balance (presets, eyedropper); vibrance, saturation; colour / B&W.
- **Profile**: look and camera profile, with an Amount slider. See [Colour, grading and profiles](../colour/).
- **Tone Curve**: point curve (RGB and per channel), parametric, targeted adjustment on the image.
- **Color Mixer**: eight-band HSL, B&W mix, **Point Color**.
- **Color Grading**: see [Colour, grading and profiles](../colour/).
- **Detail**: sharpening, noise reduction, AI denoise, super resolution.
- **Effects**: texture, clarity, dehaze, vignette, grain.
- **Calibration**: shadows tint and per-primary hue and saturation.
- **Retouch**, **Lens Corrections**, **Transform**.
- **HDR** headroom controls for HDR displays.
- Double-click a slider to reset it. Every slider is also a command.
- **Before / After** shows the difference; **Soft proof** (`S`) previews the output colour space.

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  The dock groups (Light, Colour, Tone, Detail, Optics, Effects) hold the common sliders; each **more** button opens the full section above.
  Before/After split views and zoom: **View** menu. Copy, paste and sync settings: **Photo ▸ Develop Settings**.
</Aside>
