- **History**: every change is a step, newest on top. Click a step to look at it; later steps stay until you make a new edit.
- **Step menu**: copy the step to Before, create a version from it, **Clear History**.
- **Undo** and **Redo**: **Edit** menu, `⌘Z` / `⇧⌘Z`.
- **Snapshots** are called **Versions**: **Create Version** (**Photo ▸ Develop Settings**, or the + in the panel). **Named** and **Auto** lists; click to restore, rename, update or delete.
- **Virtual copies**: **Photo ▸ Virtual Copy** (Library: the photo context menu) makes another set of settings for the same file ("Copy N" badge, stacked with the original). A real copy with its own file is **Photo ▸ File ▸ Duplicate**.
- **Reset**: **Reset** at the bottom right, **Reset Edits** (`⇧⌘R`). Double-click a slider to reset it; a section header's reset button resets the section. The eye on a section header turns it off.
- **Auto**: `⇧A` calibrated on Lightroom's Auto for 24 Sony raws.

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  Undo and redo are in the top-left. History and Versions: **Window ▸ Panels**; **Photo ▸ Develop Settings** for versions.
</Aside>
