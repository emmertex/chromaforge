- **Sharpening**: **Amount**, **Radius**, **Detail**, **Masking**. Radius and Detail need process V4 or later: [Process versions](../process-versions/).
- **Noise Reduction**: **Luminance**, **Detail**, **Contrast**; **Color**, **Detail**, **Smoothness**. Same look in grid, loupe, 1:1 and export.
- **Hot pixel removal**: a box under Noise Reduction (outliers); raw hot, dead and stuck pixels are also fixed on decode. See [Sensor and plug-ins](../sensor-and-plugins/).
- **AI Denoise** and **Super Resolution**, **Raw Details**: [Denoise](../../ai/denoise/), [Super resolution](../../ai/super-resolution/).
- Local sharpness and noise belong to [masks](../masking/).

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  **Detail** in the dock holds Sharpening, Radius, Detail, Masking and both reductions; **Detail** under it opens the full section.
</Aside>
