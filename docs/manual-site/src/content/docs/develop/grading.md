---
title: Colour grading and curves
description: Shape colour in shadows, midtones and highlights.
---

- Three colour wheels: **shadows**, **midtones**, **highlights**, plus global blend and balance.
- **Tone curve**: point curve and parametric.
- **Camera profiles** and **LUTs** set the starting look.
- **Scopes**: histogram, parade, waveform and vectorscope.
