## Two grading modes

Both edit the same recipe, so presets, history and XMP are shared. Switch in **Edit ▸ Settings ▸ General ▸ Grading Mode** or **Window ▸ Grading Mode**.

### Witchcraft (default)
Resolve-style grading: the Edit panel runs Basic, **Primaries**, **HSL Curves**.

<Shot name="grading-witchcraft" alt="Witchcraft Primaries wheels" />

- **Primaries, Colour tab**: Lift, Gamma, Gain, Offset wheels.
- **Primaries, Log tab**: Shadows, Midtones, Highlights, Offset wheels, plus Low Range and High Range.
- Wheel drags: Shift keeps hue, Cmd/Ctrl keeps saturation, Alt is fine, double-click resets.

<Shot name="grading-hsl" alt="HSL Curves" />

- **HSL Curves**: Hue vs Hue, Hue vs Sat, Hue vs Lum, with a picker to plant a point from the photo.

### Eboda (Lightroom-style)
<Shot name="grading-eboda" alt="Color Grading wheels" />

- **Color Grading**: 3-way, Shadows, Midtones, Highlights, Global; Blending and Balance.

## Other colour tools
- **Tone Curve**: point (RGB or per channel) and parametric, presets, import and export.
- **Color Mixer**: eight-band HSL and B&W mix.
- **Point Color**: pick a colour, shift its hue, saturation and luminance, with range and variance.
- **Calibration**: shadows tint, primary hue and saturation.
- **White balance**: presets and eyedropper.
- **Local colour**: Color Mixer, Point Color, curves and Colour Range inside masks.

## Profiles and looks
<Shot name="profiles" alt="The Profile Browser" menu="Window ▸ Edit Panels ▸ Profile Browser" />

- **Profile Browser** and **Profile Manager**: hide, rename, group, reorder.
- Built-in raw and creative looks, with an Amount slider.
- **Import** `.cube` LUT profiles; **export** a look as a LUT.
- **Camera profiles**: import, use, export DCP profiles; per-camera-body defaults.
- **Create a camera profile** from a colour chart (ColorChecker).
- **Camera colour**: Auto or Colorimetric calibration.

## Presets
<Shot name="presets" alt="The Presets panel" />

- Apply with an Amount slider; create, manage, import (`.lcpreset`, XMP, `.lrtemplate`).

## Colour spaces and display
- **Soft proofing** (`S`).
- Export in sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB, ProPhoto or Rec. 2020.
- **Monitor ICC** profile in Settings ▸ Display.
- Print colour management.

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  **Colour** in the dock holds the basic sliders; its **more** buttons open Color Mixer, Color Grading and Calibration, and the grading card follows the chosen mode.
  Profile Browser: **Window ▸ Edit Panels**. Copy, paste and manage presets and camera profiles: **Photo ▸ Develop Settings**. Grading Mode: **Window ▸ Grading Mode**.
</Aside>
