title: Process versions

  order: 15
---

- Every photo remembers the **process** its settings were made with. New looks ship as a new process, so existing edits never change by themselves.
- **Update to Current Process** (**Photo ▸ Develop Settings**) moves a photo to the latest, **V9**; **Reset** also does.
- V2: Highlights and Shadows keep fine detail. V3: Dehaze. V4: Sharpening Radius and Detail. V5: bright saturated colours keep hue. V6: AI Denoise amount. V7: Grain. V8: dithered 8-bit output, a gentler highlight roll-off, joint colour noise reduction. V9: Calibration primary Saturation no longer bends hue (blues).
- V1 is frozen. Bug fixes land in place; deliberate changes of look do not.
