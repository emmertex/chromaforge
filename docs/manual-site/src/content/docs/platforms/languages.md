title: Languages
description: Interface languages the app ships.
sidebar:
  order: 6
---

Set under **Settings ▸ General ▸ Language**; the choice is remembered.

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, German, Russian, French, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech, Italian, Georgian, Swedish, Finnish, Indonesian and Vietnamese.

- Menus, settings, editing, import and export, and progress messages are translated.
- Dates and numbers follow the language.
- Chinese and Japanese text needs the CJK fonts that releases include; a build from source without them shows missing glyphs for those languages.
