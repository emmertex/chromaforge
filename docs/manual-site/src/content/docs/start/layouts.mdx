import Shot from '../../../components/Shot.astro';

- **Classic**: module bar on top, panels left and right. The default.
- **Modern**: larger controls for touch and tablets.
- **Compact**: Classic, drawn denser: one-line slider rows, tighter spacing.
- Switch in **Settings ▸ General ▸ Layout**. The open photo and panel stay put.
- Pick a layout tab on any screenshot here; every screenshot follows your choice.
