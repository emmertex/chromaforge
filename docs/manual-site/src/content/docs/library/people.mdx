- **People** view (**View ▸ People**): a card per named person (face close-up, photo count, name), filtered by the current filter chips. Click one to see that person's photos.
- Names written to XMP by other apps (Lightroom, digiKam) are read as face regions.
- **Face boxes** in the loupe (**View ▸ Face Boxes**): resize, rename or remove one (**Remove Face Box**).
- **Detect Faces** (**Library** menu) finds faces; the model is an opt-in download (**Settings ▸ Faces**), nothing ships bundled.
- **Name Faces**: unnamed faces are grouped (**Group Unnamed Faces**); type a name once for a group. With an optional recognition model, **Recognize People** suggests names.
- **Right-click an unnamed face** to select its whole look-alike group, then name or ignore the group at once; the menu can also ignore that face alone.
- **Ignore** a face; the ignored list can be reviewed.
- **Unnamed faces**, and on a person's page their faces and the suggested faces, follow the year, folder or album picked in the sidebar.
- The **review queue** (suggestions, accept, reject) works with automatic background face recognition turned off.
- **Settings ▸ Faces** shows the face detector's status and device (GPU or CPU), loaded like the recognition model.
- **Faces** filter in the filter bar and **People** metadata column.
- **Roles** (**Set Role…** in the Masking panel's People component; **Who…** in the component's menu shows that row): bind a role such as *Bride* to a person per folder, collection or date range, so a mask follows them. Roles stay in the library only.
- **Forget This Person** and **Forget All Roles and Appearance Data** erase stored data.
- Face data never leaves your machine.

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  **View ▸ People** and **Library ▸ Detect Faces** in the ☰ menu.
