- **Stars**: `1`–`5`, `0` clears. `[` / `]` lower or raise by one.
- **Flags**: `P` pick, `X` reject, `U` clear, backtick cycles; `Cmd/Ctrl+↑/↓` raises or lowers the flag.
- **Colour labels**: `6` red, `7` yellow, `8` green, `9` blue. Purple and clear: **Photo ▸ Set Color Label** or the right-click menu.
- **Shift** + any of these also moves to the next photo. **Auto Advance** (**Photo** menu) does it every time.
- **Label sets**: *Colors* and *Review*, or your own (**Edit Label Names…**); names are saved in XMP.
- Bottom bar: stars, colour dots and flag buttons for the selection.
- Applies to every selected photo; one undo step.
- **Painter** (`Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+K`) paints a rating, flag or label by clicking photos.
- **Assisted Culling…** (**Library** menu): focus score, clipping and burst grouping; reject below a threshold and pick the sharpest of each burst.
  - It also suggests 1–5 stars and a Pick / Reject flag per photo from its focus, exposure and quality scores.
  - The dialog applies suggestions only to photos you have not rated or flagged yourself; one undo step.
  - Suggested stars are stored with the photo; the smart-collection field **Suggested Stars** filters on them (see [Collections](../collections/)). **Reject Closed and Blurry Eyes** is a separate command.
- **Find Similar Photos** (**Library** menu or photo menu): look-alikes by composition and tones, most similar first.
- **Delete Rejected** removes every rejected photo from the catalog.
- All keys are remappable in **Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts**; see [Shortcuts](../shortcuts/).

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  Flag and stars are on the screen's bottom bar. Labels, Assisted Culling, Find Similar: **Photo** and **Library** menus.
</Aside>
