<NeedsImage what="The Find Duplicates window: groups of copies with the best copy first and unmarked, the rest marked, and the Remove, Reject and Stack Groups buttons." />

- **Find Duplicates…** (**Library** menu): groups photos that are copies of one another across the whole library.
- **Exact copies** (same file) are found at once from the content hashes the catalog keeps.
- **Find Similar Copies** reads every photo's picture in the background (a raw by its camera JPEG, a clip by a poster frame) and also finds copies that differ: resized, re-compressed or renamed. A different photo of the same scene is not a copy. Progress and Stop show in the activity stack.
- **Raw + JPEG pairs** of the same shot are their own kind: offered as something to stack, never marked for removal.
- The best copy of each group comes first and is unmarked; the rest start marked. Click a photo to change its mark.
- **Remove** sends the marked photos to Recently Deleted (one undo step). **Reject** flags them. **Stack Groups** stacks each group; see [Stacks](../stacks/).
- Nothing is deleted from disk by the window.
- Photos in Recently Deleted, virtual copies and photos only browsed in Local never group.
- Not in the web build. Command: `library.findDuplicates` (`similar` for the second scan).

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  **Library ▸ Find Duplicates…** in the ☰ menu.
</Aside>
