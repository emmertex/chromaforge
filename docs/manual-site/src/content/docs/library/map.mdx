- **Map** module (module bar, or **Window ▸ Modules ▸ Map**): pan, wheel or double-click to zoom, zoom buttons, scale bar.
- **Map Style**: OpenStreetMap and user-added XYZ tile servers (**Add Tile Server**); tiles are cached, only visible ones are fetched.
- **Location Filter**: **Visible on Map**, **Tagged**, **Untagged**, **None**, or a **Saved Location**, with counts. It filters the filmstrip too. Click a filmstrip thumbnail to centre the map on its photo, keeping the zoom (it glides to a nearby pin); double-click to frame the pin and zoom in.
- **Pins** cluster per zoom level with counts; click selects; hover shows a thumbnail.
- **Geotag**: drag photos from the filmstrip or grid onto the map, drag pins to move them, or right-click ▸ **Geotag Selected Photos Here**. One undo step each.
- **Saved Locations**: name, centre and radius; **private** locations export without GPS and place fields. Drag a location's centre dot to move it; click the dot and drag the handle on its circle's edge to change the radius (one undo step each; **Lock Markers** holds both). Right-click the map ▸ **New Location Here…**, double-click a centre dot or click **Edit…** in the list for the **Edit Location** panel: name, radius (m or km), **Private**, Save, Cancel and Delete. Nothing is saved until **Save**, and a name another location already has is refused. Drop photos from the filmstrip on a location in the **Saved Locations** list to place them at its centre. The box at the start of each row shows whether the selected photos are inside the location (ticked: all of them; a dash: some); tick it to move the selected photos to the location's centre. A 🔒 marks a private location.
- **Search** box: latitude, longitude, a saved location or a place name (asks consent before using an online service).
- **Auto-Tag from Tracklog…** (**Metadata** menu): a GPX file sets GPS by capture time, interpolated along the track. The camera time zone you give (or the Map's **Camera time zone** field, which **Set Offset from Active Photo** fills) reads the camera's clock in that zone, even when the photo recorded a zone of its own; left empty, the photo's recorded zone is used, else UTC. The Map's **Auto-Tag Selected Photos** always uses that field once it is set, so with phone and camera photos mixed, a set field overrides the phone photos' correct recorded zones too: clear it, or tag the groups separately. Photos whose capture time the zone can't place (a leap second) are skipped and counted in the result.
- **Fill Location from GPS**: city, state, country from coordinates (offline GeoNames or an online server; opt-in).
- The Metadata panel's *Location* view edits GPS and place fields in the Library.
