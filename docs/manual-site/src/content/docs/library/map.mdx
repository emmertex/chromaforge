- **Map** module (module bar, or **Window ▸ Modules ▸ Map**): pan, wheel or double-click to zoom, zoom buttons, scale bar.
- **Map Style**: OpenStreetMap and user-added XYZ tile servers (**Add Tile Server**); tiles are cached, only visible ones are fetched.
- **Location Filter**: **Visible on Map**, **Tagged**, **Untagged**, **None**, or a **Saved Location**, with counts. It filters the filmstrip too.
- **Pins** cluster per zoom level with counts; click selects; hover shows a thumbnail.
- **Geotag**: drag photos from the filmstrip or grid onto the map, drag pins to move them, or right-click ▸ **Geotag Selected Photos Here**. One undo step each.
- **Saved Locations**: name, centre and radius; **private** locations export without GPS and place fields.
- **Search** box: latitude, longitude, a saved location or a place name (asks consent before using an online service).
- **Auto-Tag from Tracklog…** (**Metadata** menu): a GPX file sets GPS by capture time, interpolated along the track. The camera's time zone is read from the photo or asked.
- **Fill Location from GPS**: city, state, country from coordinates (offline GeoNames or an online server; opt-in).
- The Metadata panel's *Location* view edits GPS and place fields in the Library.
