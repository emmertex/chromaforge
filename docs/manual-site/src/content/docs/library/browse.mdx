- **Photo Grid** and **Square Grid**: thumbnails with badges (flag, stars, edited, keywords, stack count, virtual copy). Size slider in the bottom bar, or `=` / `-`.
- **Grid View Style** (**View** menu): compact or expanded cells, index numbers, badges, caption (file name, exposure, capture date). **Grid Info** toggles the info overlay.
- **Loupe** (**Detail View**, `D`, or Enter on a photo): one photo with the Navigator, zoom (Fit, Fill, 1:1) and filmstrip.
- **Compare** (`Shift+C`): two photos side by side, *Select* and *Candidate*, zoom linked. **Swap** and **Make Select** in the bar.
- **Survey** (`N`): the selection together, one cell each.
- **Reference View** (`Shift+R`): a reference photo beside the active one (**Photo ▸ Set as Reference Photo**).
- **People** view: faces grouped by person, see [People and faces](../people/).
- Move with arrows, `Home` / `End`; `Shift` + arrows extend the selection; **Select Only Active Photo** narrows it.
- The header shows "1 selected" when one photo is selected.
- Resizing the filmstrip keeps the active photo in place.
- **Auto Advance** (**Photo** menu) steps forward after each rating or flag.
- Cull with stars, flags and labels: [Ratings, flags, labels](../ratings/). **Assisted Culling** scores focus and bursts.
- **Quick Develop** panel (right): Saved Preset, Crop Ratio, Treatment, White Balance, Auto Tone and stepped tone buttons for the selection.
- **Sync Metadata** and **Sync Settings** buttons sit under the right panel.
- **Photo Merge** (HDR, panorama, focus stack) and **Edit In** an external editor: **Photo** menu.
