- A **library** is a folder holding the catalog; one library is open at a time, and a second program is refused with who has it.
- **File ▸ Libraries ▸ New Library…** (name, then location), **Open Library…**, **Open Recent Library** (last eight; missing ones greyed).
- **Settings ▸ General ▸ At launch**: reopen the last library or ask which. **Choose Library…** shows the chooser.
- **Back Up Library…** and **Restore Library from Backup…**. **Test Library Integrity** and **Optimise** are in the same menu.
- **Edit ▸ Library Settings…**: backup schedule (never, every exit, daily, weekly, monthly) and folder, integrity test, standard preview size, discard 1:1 previews.
- **Export as Library…**: selected or all photos, with or without originals and previews.
- **Import from Another Library…**: merge it back, with a change preview.
- Older catalogs are upgraded in place after a verified copy in `backups/`; a failure leaves the old version intact.
- Scale: hundreds of thousands of photos open in about a second.
- Everything is undoable; catalog data is separate from your files, which are never changed without your say.
- **Previews** (**Library ▸ Previews**): build standard or 1:1 previews of selected photos, discard 1:1 previews.
