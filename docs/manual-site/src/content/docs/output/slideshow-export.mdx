- Buttons at the bottom of the module: **Export Video…**, **Export PDF…**, **Export JPEG…**; also **Window ▸ Slideshow ▸ Export Slideshow…**.
- **PDF**: a page per slide with duration and fade transitions for a viewer's presentation mode.
- **JPEG**: `name-001.jpg`, `-002.jpg`… into a folder.
- **Video**: H.264 + AAC in MP4, or ProRes in MOV; includes fades, pan and zoom, overlays, titles and the mixed music.
- Playback and export use the same renderer, so they match.
- Runs in the background (activity stack); a cancelled export leaves no partial file; unreadable photos are skipped and named.
- 🟡 Other codecs and saving a slideshow with an album are not built.
