- Open: **Slideshow** in the module bar. Plays the photos in the filmstrip ("Use: All Filmstrip Photos").
- **Template Browser** (left): Default, Caption and Rating, Crop to Fill, Exif Metadata, Simple, Widescreen. **Save Template** keeps your own.
- **Saved Slideshows**: **Create Saved Slideshow** stores it as a collection.
- **Options**: Zoom to Fill Frame, Stroke Border, Cast Shadow (opacity, offset, radius, angle).
- **Layout**: Show Guides, margins linked (**Link All**) or per side, aspect preview (Screen and others).
- **Overlays**: Identity Plate, Rating Stars, Watermark text, text overlays with tokens (**Add Text**, **Tokens**), on the photo or the slide, with shadow.
- **Backdrop**: Background Color, Color Wash, Background Image with opacity.
- **Titles**: **Intro Screen** and **Ending Screen** (colour, identity plate or text).
- **Playback**: Manual Slideshow, slide and fade durations, fade colour, Random Order, Repeat Slideshow, Pan and Zoom, Draft Quality.
- **Music**: **Soundtrack** (WAV, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis; **Add**), **Fit to Music**, Volume, Balance. 🟡 MP3 and AAC are not decoded.
- Bottom bar: **Preview** (in the module), **Play** (full screen; ⌥⌘↩, also **Window ▸ Slideshow ▸ Slideshow**).

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  Modern has no Slideshow button. Use **Window ▸ Modules ▸ Slideshow**, **Window ▸ Slideshow ▸ Slideshow** to play and **Window ▸ Slideshow ▸ Slideshow Options…** for the settings.
