- **Print Job** panel (bottom of the right column): **Print to** Printer, **PDF File** or **JPEG File**.
- **Draft Mode Printing**, **Print Resolution** 72–720 ppi, **Print Sharpening** (Low / Standard / High for Glossy or Matte), **16 Bit Output** (PDF; JPEG pages stay 8-bit and say so).
- **Color Management**: **Profile** Managed by Printer or a printer / paper ICC profile (RGB only; CMYK is refused), **Intent** Perceptual or Relative with **Black Point Compensation**.
- **Print Adjustment**: Brightness and Contrast of the print only, not your edits.
- **Print to File…** writes the file; **Print One** prints a single copy; **Copies** up to 999.
- Printers: **Find Printers** lists the system's (Windows spooler) and IPP / CUPS printers. `ipps://` works over TLS; a self-made certificate is shown with its fingerprint and a **Trust Certificate** button.
- Per printer, when it reports them: Paper Type, Paper Tray, Two-Sided, Print Quality.
- 🟡 Vendor-specific driver options are set in the printer's own preferences.
- Runs in the background and shows in the activity stack.
