- Open: **Map** in the module bar.
- Pan by dragging; wheel or double-click zooms around the pointer; **+** / **−** buttons; scale bar.
- **Search place or lat, lon** (top right): coordinates, saved locations, or Nominatim after you consent.
- **Map Style**: OpenStreetMap and others; **Add Tile Server** adds your own XYZ URL; the tile cache size shows with **Clear**.
- **Location Filter** bar: **Visible on Map**, **Tagged**, **Untagged**, **None**, **Saved Location**; it filters the filmstrip too, with counts.
- **Pins** group into clusters with counts; click selects, hover previews. Click a filmstrip thumbnail to bring the map to its photo at the same zoom (it glides there when the pin is nearby); double-click to frame its pin and zoom in.
- **Geotag**: drag photos from the filmstrip onto the map, drag a pin to move its photos, or right-click ▸ geotag; type **GPS** as "latitude, longitude" in the side panel.
- **Metadata** side panel: GPS, Sublocation, City, State / Region, Country, Country Code, Title, Headline, Caption, Keywords.
- **Saved Locations**: name, centre, radius circle, photo counts; stored in the library. Drag a centre dot to move a location; the active one (clicked on the map or in the list) has a handle on its edge to drag the radius. Right-click ▸ **New Location Here…**, **New Location at Map Centre…**, a double-click on a centre dot or **Edit…** open the **Edit Location** panel (name, radius in m or km, Private; Save, Cancel, Delete), which refuses a name already in use. Photos dropped from the filmstrip on a location in the list are placed at its centre; the box at the start of a row is ticked when the selected photos are inside (a dash when some are), and ticking it moves them there. A 🔒 marks a private location.
- **Tracks**: draw GPX / KML / GeoJSON track logs; **Auto-Tag Selected Photos** by capture time with a clock offset from a photo at a known point. A set offset applies to every selected photo, overriding zones the photos recorded. Also **Metadata ▸ Auto-Tag from Tracklog…** (GPX).
- **Reverse geocoding** (off by default): offline GeoNames fills city, region and country.
- 🟡 Tiles come from the network (none offline); no animated zoom.
