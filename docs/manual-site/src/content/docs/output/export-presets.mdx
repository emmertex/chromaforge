- **Preset column** (left of the Export sheet): built-ins plus your own; **Add**, **Remove**, **Rename**, **Import…**, **Export…**.
- Built-ins: JPEG (Small) 2048 px, JPEG (Large) full size, Original + Settings, DNG, Instagram Feed 1080 px, Instagram Story / Reel 1920 px, Web / Portfolio 2048 px (copyright-only metadata), Full Resolution Master (P3).
- A preset keeps format, quality, size limit, sizing, sharpening, naming, folder and subfolder, metadata and watermark.
- Share presets as `.lcexport` files; Lightroom Classic export `.lrtemplate` files import too (settings without a counterpart are listed in a report).
- **File ▸ Export with Preset ▸ …**: exports at once with a saved preset, no sheet.
- **File ▸ Export with Previous** (⌥⇧⌘E): repeats the last export; the sheet is also prefilled with those options.
- 🟡 No HDR presets yet.

<Aside type="note" title="Modern layout">
  **File ▸ Export with Preset** and **Export with Previous** are in the ☰ menu.
</Aside>
