## Highlights

- **Contributions from OneSlips:** a large body of work from OneSlips' fork, ported by hand into Chromaforge. Thanks
  @OneSlips. (no PR)
  - **Masks and people:** People masks on Google's open MediaPipe models, Object masks on SAM 2.1, an optional EoMT
    scene model for Sky, Landscape and People, remembered objects and people's looks, Lightroom Classic's brush,
    linked masks, next-mask settings, Point Color and Color Mixer per mask, masks that follow lens corrections and
    Upright, and an eye focus strip for culling.
  - **Remove and retouch:** Generative Fill with your own API key, content-aware Fill with grain and seamless edges,
    hot, dead and stuck pixel repair, Remove Dust with dust maps, and a Retouch section (Smooth Skin, Find Blemishes,
    Teeth).
  - **GPU and speed:** more of the render, spots and thumbnails on the graphics card, drafts drawn straight from it, a
    second graphics card for exports and previews, faster and cancellable exports, Test my machine, smoother editing
    and previews that stay.
  - **Library and import:** an Import tab (the new default; the import review stays one setting away), Collections,
    Show Alternatives, live culling counts, naming presets, Delete Rejected Photos and copy-first catalog upgrades.
  - **Presets and profiles:** presets shared by every library, a Presets Manager, per-setting presets, Lightroom XMP
    preset export, a Profile Manager, camera profiles per body and from a colour chart, a Canon EOS R5 C profile and
    particle film grain.
  - **Interface:** an opt-in Compact layout, and Logitech MX Creative Console and Loupedeck+ control surfaces.
