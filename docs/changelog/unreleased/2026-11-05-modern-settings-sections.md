## Interface

- **Modern Settings follows the tab row:** the Modern layout's Settings rail has one section per Settings-window tab
  (General, Import, Folders, Performance, Display, Interface, Slideshow, Faces, Profiles, AI Denoise, AI Models,
  Generative Fill, Shortcuts, Controls, Connections, Sync), in the tab row's order, each showing exactly that tab's
  settings. The grouped sections (Library, Editing, Display & Speed) are gone.
