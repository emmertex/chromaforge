## Develop and masking

- **Lens Blur:** a Lens Blur section in the Edit panel (Apply, Blur Amount, Boost, Transition, In Focus: the photo's subject or any mask, Find Subject on SAM 3, Brush Refinement, a focus shape that moves and resizes on the photo) and a Lens Blur block in every mask. Disc-shaped bokeh on the processor for both renderers. The falloff follows the distance in the picture from the focus, not the depth of the scene: there is no depth map yet. Catalog format 22. Ported from upstream, by @czarist.
