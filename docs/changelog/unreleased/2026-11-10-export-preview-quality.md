## Export

- **Sharper preview:** the Export module's preview requests a thumbnail at the size it is drawn, so a single photo filling the preview area is no longer a 256-pixel thumbnail scaled up.
- **From Develop, the open photo:** entering the Export or Print module from Develop now selects the photo open on screen (Develop's filmstrip can hold a multi-selection while another photo is stepped to); a selection made outside Develop is kept.
