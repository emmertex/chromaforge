## Import

- **Capture One develop settings:** the Capture One catalog import brings each variant's exposure, crop and its aspect, orientation and straighten angle, flips and white balance (Capture One's change from as shot, an approximation, marked as such for raws edited on the relative white balance scale); every other setting a photo uses is named in the result window per Capture One tool with its photo count, and photos are dated at their last change in Capture One where the catalog records it.
