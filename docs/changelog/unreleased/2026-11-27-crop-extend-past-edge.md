## Develop

- **Crop past the edge (#861):** Crop ▸ Extend Past the Edge lets the crop reach past the photo (up to half its size on each side), shown as blank canvas; Fill Edges fills it Content-Aware (on this computer) or Generative (your own generative fill service and key). Commands `crop.extend`, `crop.fillEdges`, `crop.clearFill`, `crop.cancelFill`.
