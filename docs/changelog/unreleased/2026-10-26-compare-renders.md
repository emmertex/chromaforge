## Colour

- **Compare renders from any editor:** `compare REFERENCE TEST` in the command-line tool registers two renders of one photo (from the app, Lightroom, Capture One, darktable or a camera) and reports CIEDE2000 overall, by tone band and by hue, with lightness, chroma and hue bias, a `--json` report and a `--diff` ΔE00 map.
