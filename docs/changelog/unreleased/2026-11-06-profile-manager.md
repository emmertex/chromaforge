## Interface

- **Profile Manager:** a window of its own (Photo ▸ Develop Settings ▸ Manage Profiles…, **Manage…** in the profile browser, or Settings ▸ Profiles) lists every look, LUT profile and camera profile in the Presets Manager's table: your camera profiles (.dcp) by camera model, your LUT groups, the built-in looks and the bundled camera profiles. Hide / Show (also for camera profiles, which then leave the camera profile choices), Rename and Group for looks and LUTs, Favorite, Import Profiles… and Import Folder…, and **Delete** for your own .dcp and LUT files, asked in the row; built-ins are hidden, not deleted. Each row says how many photos use it and which camera bodies start with it; photos using a deleted profile keep their settings and fall back safely (a deleted camera profile gives the camera's default). The window fits the screen and keeps its size. The profile browser's **None (camera profile only)** takes the creative profile off in one click (`dialog.profileManager`, `profile.delete`). Based on work by @OneSlips.
