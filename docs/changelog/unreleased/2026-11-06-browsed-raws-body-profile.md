## Import

- **Browsed raws show their body's camera profile:** raws browsed in the Import tab (shown in place, not yet imported)
  render with their camera body's own camera profile, or the Import panel's choice for the body, exactly as importing
  them will apply it, with nothing written to the photo or its sidecar. A body profile set after the folder was
  browsed shows at once. Raws catalogued before serial numbers were kept are known by the serial their file states,
  and a renamed file's leading number (`10-…`) is no longer taken for a camera body. Thanks @OneSlips.
