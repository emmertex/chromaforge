## Interface

- **Tool panels give the panel back:** closing Crop, Remove, Masking or Red Eye restores the panel shown before (and its scroll position), or keeps it hidden; a drag on a zoomed photo in Masking with no tool armed pans it. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Crop tool:** new photos start with Constrain Crop on (Settings ▸ Import can turn it off); whole crop edges resize their side; X keeps an untouched photo's shown lock. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
