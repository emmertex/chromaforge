## Interface

- **Settings is a window with one row of tabs:** in the Classic layout Settings opens as a resizable window with
  one tab per page (General, Import, Folders, Performance, Display, Interface, Slideshow, Faces, Profiles, AI
  Denoise, AI Models, Generative Fill, Shortcuts, Controls, Connections, Sync), the tabs that don't fit under More.
  It keeps its size and place on every tab and opens again at the size and on the tab it was left at; Ctrl+Tab
  steps through the tabs. Every setting keeps its control. The Modern layout keeps the full-window Settings. Thanks
  @OneSlips.
