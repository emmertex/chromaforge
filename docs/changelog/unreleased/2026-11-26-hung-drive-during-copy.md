## Import

- **A hung drive during Copy or Move:** copies run per drive (each copy holds a place on its card and on its destination, starting with as many copies as before, then each drive finding its own number), so a card or destination that stops answering fails only its own files — "volume not responding", or "outcome unknown" for a copy that was under way, which may still appear in the destination folder outside the library; the original is always kept. Cancel returns even while a drive hangs; a raw waiting for Copy as DNG when you cancel keeps its raw copy.
