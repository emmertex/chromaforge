## Library

- **Culling and browsing from OneSlips:** Caps Lock turns Auto Advance on while it is on; Photo ▸ Delete Rejected Photos
  (`library.deleteRejected`, `files: keep | recycle`) takes the view's rejects in one undo step; grid thumbnails follow a
  slider drag; a panel toggled off brings back what was shown before it; auto-written XMP sidecars skip photos that are
  only browsed until they are imported (`library.xmpPreferences` `writeBrowsed`). Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
