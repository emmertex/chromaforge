## Interface

- **Settings categories on the left:** the Settings window lists its categories down its left side in the Classic layout (along the top in Compact); Settings ▸ General ▸ Settings Categories switches between the two in either layout. The Layout row reads Classic, Compact, Modern.
- **Only the slider settings that apply:** Settings ▸ Interface hides the slider style where the layout fixes it (Compact, Modern) and Slider control for pills, and notes that Shift gives fine control.
- **Wider What's New, simpler About:** What's New opens three times as wide (narrower only on a small window); About drops its Contributors and Models lists.
- **Web site links:** Help ▸ Chromaforge Website and Help ▸ Chromaforge Help open the Chromaforge web site (brand.toml `website` and `docs`).
