## Library

- **Map follows the filmstrip:** one click on a filmstrip thumbnail centres the map on that photo at the zoom it has, as in Lightroom Classic. The map glides there when the pin is within three map widths and jumps when it is farther; a photo without a position leaves the map alone, and a drag, zoom or search ends a glide. Before, the map only moved when the pin was off screen, or on a double-click (which still frames the pin and zooms in).
