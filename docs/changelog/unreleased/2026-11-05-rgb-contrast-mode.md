## Develop

- **RGB mode for Contrast and Blacks:** a **Contrast Mode** switch (**Luminance** | **RGB**) in **Basic ▸ Tone** lets the **Contrast** and **Blacks** sliders apply their tonal response across the Red, Green and Blue channels like an RGB tone curve, enriching shadow depth and colour contrast while keeping **Luminance** as the default. Thanks @elianos-design for the report (issue #839).
