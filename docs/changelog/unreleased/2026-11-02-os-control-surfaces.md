## Interface

- **Control surfaces:** the Logitech MX Creative Console (Keypad, Dialpad, Actions Ring) and the Loupedeck+ drive
  develop sliders, masks, brushes, ratings and presets, with pages that follow the tool, dial acceleration, one undo
  step per turn, an on-screen overlay for every change and Settings → Controls drawn around the device; a paired,
  local, surfaces-only port for the Logi Options+ plugin (`surface.*` methods). New crate `lcc-surface`; icons by
  RAD Films (MIT). Thanks @OneSlips (ported from their fork, TactileBridge). (no PR)
