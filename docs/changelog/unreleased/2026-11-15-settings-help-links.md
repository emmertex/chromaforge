## Interface

- **Help links in Settings:** a **?** beside each significant Settings group opens the online manual at its heading (`app.openHelp {topic}`; without a topic it lists them). Topics live in one table checked against the manual's headings by a test, so links can't rot. The manual gains a Settings reference page (every tab and group, the quick settings bar, eye focus strip, face scan and semantic search controls) and a Browse without importing section.
