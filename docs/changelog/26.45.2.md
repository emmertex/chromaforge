  after the photo's colour tools within the mask (CPU and GPU), with `mask.pointColorPick` / `mask.pointColorSet` /
  `mask.pointColorDelete`. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Mask thumbnails, Roles window:** thumbnails on the mask and part rows, the overlay on pin hover, and a Roles window
  (`people.roles` / `people.clearRole`). Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)

  Settings), a stale badge on AI masks not computed for this photo, and `masks.recompute` on the AI worker in the

## Performance

- **Exports take turns at the GPU, and a lost GPU comes back:** export renders share the graphics card in turns sized by its memory, behind the window's work; a readback waits only for its own work (a busy GPU sends one photo to the CPU instead of turning the GPU off), and a lost device is replaced after 3 s, 6 s, 12 s … (at most five times). Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Faster folder browsing and exports:** browsing a folder shows its first photos within about a second instead of after every file was read (2,933 CR3s: 30.6 s → 0.9 s to the first photo), exports render up to 12 photos side by side on big machines, and the automatic cache memory grows to a quarter of the RAM, up to 16 GB (was 1.5 GB). Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Faster HDR gain map exports:** the HDR rendition reuses the SDR render's decoded original and stages, and the gain map is computed on every core. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Drafts drawn straight from the GPU, two graphics cards:** the desktop app offers the window's GPU device to the renderer, so a slider drag's drafts stay on the graphics card as textures (no readback, conversion or re-upload; not yet on HDR windows; `<PREFIX>_GPU_WINDOW=0` / `=1` override). Settings ▸ Performance chooses the graphics card for editing (Automatic: the screen's) and a second graphics card that takes exports, previews built ahead and thumbnails, with a gate and memory budget of its own, and AI models make their device there; its loss leaves the editing card alone (`app.gpu` `card`, `helper`, `adapters`). Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **More of the render on the GPU:** LUT profiles, Refine Edges, mask Feather and soft proofs render on the graphics card instead of falling back to the processor. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Spots and defringe on the GPU:** Clone, Heal and Remove spots (with Remove's automatic source scoring) and Defringe render on the graphics card, so edits to a photo with spots no longer round-trip through the processor. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Faster "Limit file size":** the quality that fits is predicted from a small mosaic of the photo, so a limited JPEG takes at most two full encodes instead of a binary search, and a gain map JPEG encodes its map once. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Previews that stay:** an Automatic preview cache sized from the drive's free space (15 %, at least 20 GB where the drive has room, never more than half of what is free); the loupe shows a built preview instead of rendering it again; CR3 thumbnails decode the 1620 px PRVW copy and camera-preview thumbnails are kept on disk. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Smoother editing:** a still photo refines to the full render while a slider or control-surface gesture stays open; drafts no longer wait for a photo's first full render (one shared raw decode); masks keep their planes between slider ticks; zoomed brushed masks render in ~150 ms instead of seconds; presets, catalog flags and GPU uploads never block the window; renders leave cores free at below-normal priority. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Test my machine:** Settings ▸ Performance ▸ Exports sets the photos exported side by side and the GPU for exports, or measures this computer and keeps the fastest settings (with the GPU and the processor together when faster); GPU failures and restarts are shown. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Camera previews first while culling:** the embedded camera JPEG is the first picture of every raw in the grid, filmstrip and loupe (a stand-in for edited raws); stand-ins get two render workers of their own, background thumbnail renders run at most two at once, and background work waits while a picture on screen is missing. Ported from @OneSlips (PERF-CULL-TIERS). (no PR)

Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Linked masks:** masks applied to several photos in one go stay linked; with Auto Sync on an edit reaches the copies on the selected photos (hand-made parts exactly, AI parts keep their own selection). Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Settings for the next mask:** with no mask selected, the sliders and effect menu set what the next new mask starts with (`mask.next`, Reset settings on new mask); a tool's last effect no longer starts a new mask. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Colour and Luminance Range pickers:** Color Range adds (⇧), removes (⌥) and drags its samples and picks the main colours of a dragged area; Luminance Range picks its tones from the photo (`mask.colorPin`, `mask.sampleLuminance`). Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **Remembered objects and people's looks:** `concepts.teach` / `concepts.find` remember a thing selected on one photo and find it on others (SAM 2.1), and a `concept` mask kind is the recipe presets and Auto Sync find again on each photo; `people.learn` remembers how named people look, so People masks by name or role find them where nobody named them; renamed masks keep their names in user presets; the edited component and an armed brush carry to a linked mask's copy; Reset Settings on New Mask is saved. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **People masks on the people models:** `mask.add {kind: "people", person?, parts?, who?}`; the selection is computed per photo by MediaPipe Selfie Multiclass + Face Landmarker (Apache-2.0) on the AI worker, one analysis per photo shared by every part, and stored with the mask (presets and Auto Sync carry the recipe, never the bitmap). Falls back to the heuristic without the models. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
- **People and Remembered in Create New Mask:** a People picker (everyone or a face found on the photo, by name, and the parts), a Remembered picker and Remember as… on a mask; per-person masks on large groups (each small person on their own crop); a People mask that finds nobody selects nothing; `people.recognize` and `people.cluster`. - **Masks are judged while painted:** a brush stroke or handle drag on a mask with settings hides every mask overlay while it runs; O turns off any overlay, All Mask Overlays included, which a pill on the photo now announces and which is no longer kept between sessions. Thanks @OneSlips. (no PR)
