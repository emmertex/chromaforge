# 26.48.1

## Develop and masking

- **Clipping previews on a mask's sliders:** holding `Alt` (`Option` on macOS) while dragging a mask's Exposure, Highlights, Whites, Shadows or Blacks slider in Masking shows the same clipping preview as the Edit panel's tone sliders (red where highlights clip, blue where shadows clip). The mask's tint, outlines and pins step aside while the preview shows, and the `J` clipping overlay yields to it.
- **Recolor on a mask:** the masked area's main colour takes a picked colour and keeps its own shading, folds and light; only the dominant colour changes, with a Range to widen or narrow the hue window and a Color to Replace pick for two-colour areas. Commands `mask.recolor` and `mask.recolorPick` drive it from the CLI, MCP and scripts.
- **Lens Blur Brush Refinement:** Focus and Blur brushes paint over the estimated depth (`lensBlur.brushStroke`, `lensBlur.brushReset`).
- **Lens Blur in masks:** a mask can blur its selection or the outside, with Blur Amount and Boost (`mask.adjust`).

## Develop

- **Lens database on first run:** the desktop app fetches lensfun's lens profiles (CC BY-SA 3.0) once on its first
  run, on the activity stack; a lens picked for a photo is written to XMP as `optics.lens` (#307).
- **Masks:** Solo Mode for a mask's sections (`mask.solo`), Color Grading as its own section under Colour, distinct icons for the Colour tools, and a reticle icon for the Targeted Adjustment tool (#1305, #1301).
- **Sharpening views:** holding `Alt` (`Option` on macOS) while dragging a Sharpening slider in the Detail section shows a black-and-white view of what that slider does: the **Masking** mask (white is sharpened, black is left alone), the photo blurred at the **Radius**, the fine edges **Detail** works on, or the sharpening **Amount** applies. The view also shows on edits that do not sharpen yet.
- **Lightroom-style clipping overlay:** the `J` clipping overlay paints solid red where all three channels clip at highlights (a blown-out white) and a lighter red tint where only some channels do; likewise solid blue for an all-channel shadow clip and a lighter blue tint for a partial one. The Alt/Option-drag threshold previews keep their single-side views.
- **Glow:** a Resolve-style bloom in the Effects panel (beyond Lightroom): the bright parts above a Threshold, spread as a halo (Radius, H/V Ratio, Gain, Gamma) and composited in Screen or Add at a strength set by Glow, with Recovery keeping the bright regions' detail. Resolution independent; presets, copy/sync and the tool profiles carry it.
- **Style brushes:** a mask effect preset can start a Brush mask directly with that effect (`mask.styleBrush`, Style Brush menu in Masks; plan 7.15, gap 43).
- Tone Equalizer (beyond Lightroom, ported from darktable): exposure per luminance band from -8 EV to middle grey, through a guided-filter mask that preserves detail.
- **Lens corrections and Transform on video:** clips take Lens Corrections, Upright (Auto, Level, Vertical, Full) and the Transform sliders, applied to every frame in playback, Capture Frame and export.
- **Lens Blur Boost:** a Boost slider brightens out-of-focus highlights into bokeh discs; `lensBlur.set {boost}` and the `lensBlur.boost` develop control.

## Library

- **People face matches:** Unknown hover names the closest confirmed person and gives their similarity score; Suggested and a person's similar faces show the suggested match and score. Visually separate Unknown clusters and keep Suggested faces out of Unknown-group selection (part of #1365).
- **Suggestions across the whole library:** every unnamed face with an embedding is compared with the people you have named, a slice at a time, so a face is offered under Suggested wherever it falls in the unnamed list. Before, only the faces on the first page of the list (and the first 1500 the view puts in look-alike order) were compared, so in a large library a face that looked like a confirmed person could stay in Unknown and Suggested could look empty. `faces.unnamed` says how far the comparison has got (`scan`, and `budgetMs` asks for less work per call), its `groups` hold every suggested face the filter lets through rather than the listed page's, and the Suggested category says while it is still looking (#1365).
- **Suggested while names change:** naming a face starts the comparison again against the people you have named, and what the last comparison found stays under Suggested in the meantime, so the category does not fall empty after each new name (a suggestion for a person since renamed or removed is dropped). The count beside Suggested is the library's suggestions rather than the listed page's, and confirming or rejecting a whole group is sent 5000 faces at a time, so a person with a library's worth of look-alikes can be decided in one go (#1365).
- **Image Quality Scanner: auto-assigned ratings and flags** (issue #1362): the scanner can now write the star rating a photo's score asks for — five configurable thresholds, 20 / 40 / 55 / 70 / 85 by default — and its Pick / Reject flags (`photo.qualityScan`'s `autoRate`, `starBreaks`, `overwriteMetadata` on top of the existing `applyFlags`). A rating or flag the user set is never changed by a scan, only one an earlier scan wrote (`QualityScan::rating_written` / `flag_written`), unless **Overwrite existing** is ticked for the run; both are written in the run's single undo step and follow the XMP sidecar sync. The dialog gains **Auto-Assign Metadata** (the rating switch, the five thresholds, Overwrite existing). The pixels are measured on the multi-threaded CPU. `<cli> quality-scan --auto-rate --auto-flag --overwrite-metadata --star-breaks`; the summary reports `rated` beside `flagged`.
- **Update DNG Preview & Metadata in place:** the new preview and XMP go where the old ones were when they fit, so updating a DNG again no longer grows it; BigTIFF DNGs and directories stored out of tag order are handled.
- **Auto-Stack by Capture Time can put the raw or the JPEG on top:** a new **Put on top of each stack** choice (*First photo in time*, *Raw file*, *JPEG file*). A stack without a photo of that type keeps its first photo in time.
- **More editors in the Edit In menu:** saved Edit In presets are listed in Photo → Edit In and on the right-click menu, each with its own items (Lightroom's choices, one *Edit in* item for a raw developer, or up to 8 items with their own arguments for a denoiser). An item takes the whole selection with one start of the editor. Requested by AnitaRRC.
- **Saved locations are edited on the map:** drag a location's centre to move it and the handle on its edge to change its radius (one undo step each; Lock Markers holds them), as in Lightroom Classic. Right-click ▸ New Location Here…, a double-click or Edit… opens an Edit Location panel (name, radius in m or km, private, delete), replacing the side panel's create-only form; saving under a name already in use is refused instead of silently replacing that location. Photos dropped from the filmstrip on a location in the list are placed at its centre.
- **Plug-in publish services:** a plug-in's publish service gets the user's service and its settings, the published collection, and each photo's title, caption and keywords, and can show the site's comments, so Flickr- or SmugMug-style services can come as plug-ins.
- **Scoped face maintenance:** scan visible photos, a selection, folders or collections; rebuild embeddings or rescan while preserving named, ignored, and manual faces. Reset all face data with confirmation; the continuous scan stays paused until resumed.

## Import, export and formats

- **Export preview with HDR mode:** the Export screen previews the first photo to be exported as its file will be, rendered by the export's own path (size, output sharpening, watermark). A **SDR | HDR** choice shows an HDR export's HDR rendition on an HDR display, or its SDR rendition with a line saying why HDR isn't shown and what to turn on. The preview renders in the background and never holds up Export.
- **HDR gain map export for AVIF and JPEG XL:** with HDR output on, the new HDR mode choice (Gain map or HDR image, the default) writes an SDR image with an ISO 21496-1 gain map, or one PQ / HLG file as before. The AVIF follows the tone map derived item layout libavif writes; the JPEG XL carries libjxl's gain map box. `app.export` takes `hdrMode: "gainMap"` or `"hdr"`.
- About 400 cameras released after 2018 get real colour matrices (from LibRaw) instead of the neutral fallback when the camera JPEG fit is not used.

## Import

- **More CR3 variants decode:** Canon CRX partial subbands, multi-tile version 0x200 C-RAW and adaptive low-frequency quantization now decode from sensor data instead of falling back to the embedded preview; ported from LibRaw's `crx.cpp` (LGPL 2.1) and checked on synthetic streams, since no public file uses them.
- **More old cameras decode:** Sony DSC-F828 / DSC-V3 SRF files and Fujifilm FinePix S200EXR / S205EXR RAFs now open from the sensor data instead of the embedded preview. The F828's emerald photosites are read as green, so its colour is approximate.
- **Capture One catalogs bring more edits:** contrast, brightness, saturation, High Dynamic Range, clarity and
  structure, Levels and the RGB / red / green / blue curves now come across; settings without an equivalent are
  still listed in the result window.
- **Images up to Lightroom Classic's size:** the largest image that opens is now 512 × 2^20 pixels (536,870,912), the limit Lightroom Classic 16.0 itself enforces (it imported a 536,848,900 pixel file and refused one of 536,895,241); it was 512,000,000, which refused files Lightroom opens (#1477).

## Interface

- **Click through (macOS):** a new choice in Settings ▸ Interface makes the click that brings a background window to the front only bring it to the front, as Lightroom Classic does on the Mac. The default still passes the click on. Agents use `app.backgroundClicks`.
- **Clearer update dialog:** Help ▸ Check for Updates… shows how long ago it last checked and keeps the line current while the window is open, describes what Download does in one plain sentence (replacing the SHA-256 line), and makes Install and Relaunch the highlighted, focused default button once the download is ready.
- **What's New by date:** Help ▸ What's New has a heading per date, the newest open and the others closed, and builds only the dates in view, adding more as you scroll, so it opens and scrolls faster. Code, bold and links in release notes now render, in What's New and in Check for Updates.
- **Errors stay readable:** every failure message uses the longer error time; about 150 failures that were shown for 1.4 s as plain messages now stay for 6 s.
- **One note per change:** ratings, flags, colour labels, undo and redo, keywords added or removed, a preset applied to several photos, geotags and moves of photos or folders are said once, by the on-screen overlay; the toast that repeated them is gone in every mode.
- **Menus on a phone:** menus opened from ☰ in the Modern layout no longer run off a phone's screen; long items wrap (#1288).
- **One look for messages:** the messages at the bottom of the window (notes, failures, messages with a button) share one drawing and one duration policy by kind and text length; failures have a red edge. `ui.inspect` lists each message's `kind`.
- **Messages that stay:** a message about a condition that is still true (unsaved changes, XMP sidecars waiting, a pairing request) stays until dismissed or the condition ends. `ui.toast` raises and clears one by key; `ui.inspect` lists it as `kind: "sticky"` with its `key`.

## Performance

- **Faster raw repair passes:** the candidate scan of Remove Hot Pixels and the quick test of Fix Hot and Dead Pixels read neighbours at fixed offsets and skip the median where the excess cannot pass, about 3.5 times faster per photo with identical output (#1377). A review of the change also made the black-level spread read exactly what the correction reads, so short per-position black-level arrays are handled the same way in both.
- **Noise reduction drags on the CPU:** a Luminance / Detail drag keeps the colour noise reduction and reruns only
  luminance NR (draft 1152×768 of a 24 MP raw: 63.8 → 33.4 ms on a loaded Windows machine); release renders and
  exports are unchanged. `cargo xtask bench --strict` also fails on missed wall-time budgets.
- Large libraries: the background preparation tick is bounded (a stretch of the library a tick, previews only around the photo on screen), and the lens database is read in the background after a library opens (#979).
- **Faster Nikon High Efficiency decoding:** the inverse Star-Tetrix adds fixed-offset slices away from the image edges instead of calling a bounds-checked neighbour lookup per sample, its lifting steps update their plane in place, and each precinct's coefficients are written straight into the final bands instead of per-slice copies gathered serially; HE and HE★ raws decode 23 to 31 % faster with bit-identical output (#1377).
- **Faster binning for reduced-size raw development:** one scratch buffer per worker thread, and each colour of a row of blocks summed once when the blocks of the row share a pattern phase, with bit-identical output (#1377).

## Fixes

- **Linux AppImage:** the app registers a launcher entry and its icon for itself on first start, so the Wayland and X11 taskbars show its icon instead of the generic one. Entries from a package or a launcher tool are left alone.
- **Sync across patch releases:** a desktop and a phone on patch releases of the same release now sync; only different releases are refused, with both versions named (#1294).
- **Windows on ARM:** DirectX 12 is now the default graphics interface, with Vulkan as the fallback; the Adreno Vulkan driver could lose its device while scrolling the library (#1029).
- **Dialog sections stay closed:** a dialog body laid out directly while it fits and in a scroll area once it doesn't gave its widgets a separate state in each layout, so closing a section that took the body from too tall to short enough switched layouts, found the section open again and alternated on every frame, dropping clicks (What's New with a long newest date). The body's contents now keep one state in both layouts.
- An Exif rating of 65535 (rejected) reads as rejected, not as five stars (from craft-libs #967, upstream #1995).
- Lightroom XMP: a `rdf:parseType="Resource"` struct written with attribute fields reads like the other forms (from craft-libs #967, upstream #1226).
- Browser app: no internal error ("no pids on this platform") at start-up (#1459).

## Raw support

- Sony ARW6 (Compressed RAW 2) 12-bit mode decodes when its tile headers match the verified 14-bit variant; checked on synthetic files only.
- An uncompressed DNG whose strip byte count covers only its ActiveArea columns (DJI Mavic 3) is read in full instead of ending in black rows (from craft-libs #2042).

## Accessibility

- **Brush by keyboard:** the mask brush has a focusable handle on the photo; Return puts it down, the arrow keys paint while it is down (plan 5.8, gap 23).

## Added

- **Filmstrip filter:** filter the filmstrip by flag, rating and colour label from its header, in Lightroom Classic's order (#1296).
- **Speed-edit keys:** in Develop, hold a key and drag or scroll on the photo to move a slider (`E` Exposure, `C` Contrast, `H` Highlights, `S` Shadows, `W` Whites, `B` Blacks), one undo step per gesture; remap with `app.setSpeedKey`. Listed in Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts.
- **Camera Matching looks:** Photo ▸ Develop Settings ▸ Create Camera Matching Look from Raw + JPEG fits the camera's JPEG rendering (tone curves and a colour table) from your own raw + JPEG pairs and adds it to the Profile Browser's Camera Matching group (`profile.fitCameraLook`).
- **Lens Blur:** a depth-of-field blur rendered from the depth model (or, without it, from the subject), with a focus picker, Blur Amount, focal range and bokeh shapes; `lensBlur.set` / `lensBlur.status` commands and develop controls.
- **Skin-tone uniformity:** Hue, Saturation and Lightness Uniformity sliders on Point Color (photo and masks) pull the colours within a sample's range towards it.

## New features

- **Folder polling** reacts to the system's change notifications (FSEvents, ReadDirectoryChangesW, inotify), with the interval poll kept as the fallback (gap 37, #123).
- **One copy at a time:** a second launch hands its files and links to the running app and exits; `<PREFIX>_NEW_INSTANCE` or any option starts a separate copy.
- **Links** (`<scheme>://sync?path=…`) are registered by the Windows installer and the Linux desktop entry (not yet on macOS).
- Adaptive presets: mask recipes computed again on each photo, a built-in Smart Masks: Adaptive group, version 2 preset files; builds without the AI model apply the rest and report the skipped masks.

## New

- **Loupe and filmstrip backgrounds:** a neutral grey (or black, white, the theme's shade) round the photo and behind the filmstrip, from the loupe's right-click menu, Window ▸ Appearance or Settings ▸ Interface (#1295).
- **Highlight reconstruction methods:** Detail ▸ Sensor ▸ Highlights offers Inpaint Opposed and Guided Laplacian
  (ported from darktable) beside the default Rebuild (`sensor.highlightMode`).
- **Match Look** (Photo ▸ Develop Settings): the selected photos take the active photo's brightness, contrast and colour cast, through Exposure, Contrast and the global Color Grading tint (`develop.matchLook`).

## Packaging

- **macOS:** 26.48 is the last release with the universal `-macos-universal.dmg`; from 26.49.0 there are only `-macos-arm64.dmg` and `-macos-x64.dmg` (#1269). Installs of 26.47.x or earlier update to 26.48 through the universal image as before, and from there the updater takes the image for its Mac.
- Desktop packages (Linux, macOS, Windows, FreeBSD) build with the `dist` cargo profile (thin LTO, one codegen unit).
- The release's package-size check now also covers the Flatpak, fails on a download with no budget entry, and checks pre-releases against the release's entries.

## Building

- **Single-language builds:** building from source with `CRAFT_FONTS_LANGUAGE=<code>` embeds only the fonts that language's interface needs and offers only that language and English. Release builds are unchanged.
- **Release size check:** each release job compares its downloads with a stored size budget and fails on more than 10 % growth.

## Changes

- **Transform:** a Guided Upright icon beside the Upright modes; the active mode is named in place of the "Upright" heading, and the mode buttons no longer shift when switching modes (#1274).

## Export

- **OpenEXR export:** a new export format writes the rendered photo scene-linear as 16-bit half or 32-bit float OpenEXR (lossless ZIP), in linear Rec. 2020 or ACEScg primaries recorded in the file's `chromaticities` attribute. Agents use `app.export` with `format: "exr"`, `bitDepth` 16 or 32 and `exrPrimaries` (`rec2020` or `acescg`).

## Plug-ins

- **Plug-ins join imports:** a new `beforeImport` hook is asked about each file while an import prepares it and can import another file instead (a transcoded twin, say) or skip it (reported as `skippedByPlugin`); an `afterImport` hook is told about each committed batch with the new photos and its result is reported in the import's output as `pluginResults`. A new `library.remove` host call (permission and host feature `libraryRemove`) moves photos to Recently Deleted; the files on disk are never touched. Host features `beforeImport`, `afterImport` and `libraryRemove`; new example plug-in Import Tools.
- **Update checks leave a record:** each check logs a line (also "up to date") and the plug-in's page shows when it last ran; `plugin.list` → `updates.checkedAgo` gives the seconds.

## Features

- **Export with Several Presets:** File ▸ More Exports ▸ Export with Several Presets… (and `export.runPresets`) exports the selection with several export presets in one run, decoding each photo once (Capture One's process recipes).

## Fixed

- "Image too large" errors give the image's pixel count and the limit, 536,870,912 pixels (Lightroom Classic's "512 megapixels") (#1477).
